Rubricae generales Missarum

Missa quotidie celebrari debet secundum ordinem Officii, de quo fit illo die, idest de festo Toto Duplici, vel Duplici, vel Semiduplici, vel Simplici, vel trium Lectionum; de Dominica vel Vigilia, vel Feria, vel Octava: et extra ordinem Officii, Votiva vel pro Defunctis.

Mass is to be celebrated every day according to the order of the Office said that day, that is, of a feast Totum Duplex, Duplex, Semiduplex, Simplex or of three Lessons; of a Sunday, a Vigil, a Feria or an Octave; and, outside the order of the Office, a Votive Mass or a Mass for the Dead.

I De FestisOf Feasts

1. Festa Tota Duplicia, Duplicia, Semiduplicia, Simplicia et trium Lectionum semper celebrantur proprio die, nisi ab Officio vel festo nobiliori impediantur, ut habetur infra in Rubrica tit. VII.

1. Feasts Totum Duplex, Duplex, Semiduplex, Simplex and of three Lessons are always celebrated on their own day, unless they are impeded by a higher Office or feast, as is set out below in title VII.

2. In festis Totis Duplicibus et Duplicibus dicitur una tantum Oratio, nisi aliqua Memoria occurrat facienda, quae tamen aliquando juxta Rubricam tit. VIII omittenda est.

2. On feasts Totum Duplex and Duplex only one Prayer is said, unless some Commemoration occurs which must be made; this, however, is sometimes to be left out, according to title VIII.

3. In festis Semiduplicibus et infra, extra Octavam solemnem vel majorem, tres saltem Orationes dicantur, ut habetur infra in Rubrica tit. X.

3. On feasts Semiduplex and below, outside a solemn or greater Octave, at least three Prayers are said, as set out below in title X.

II De OctavisOf Octaves

1. Missa infra Octavas, si de ipsis fiat Officium, dicitur sicut in die, nisi pro singulis Octavae diebus Missa propria habeatur. Credo tamen quotidie infra Octavas solemnissimas tantum dicitur.

1. Within Octaves, when their Office is said, the Mass is said as on the feast itself, unless a proper Mass is given for each day of the Octave. The Credo, however, is said daily only within the most solemn Octaves.

2. Si Officium Octavae ab aliquo festo vel Dominica impediatur, in Missa festi vel Dominicae fit vel omittitur Memoria Octavae, prout in Rubricis Breviarii de singulis dicitur.

2. If the Office of the Octave is impeded by a feast or a Sunday, the Commemoration of the Octave is made or omitted in the Mass of the feast or Sunday, as the Rubrics of the Breviary say for each case.

3. Dum autem fit de Octava solemni et supra, una tantum Oratio dicatur, nisi Memoria facienda sit. Per Octavam simplicem et in die Octava trium Lectionum plures Orationes dicendae sunt, prout habetur in Rubrica tit. X.

3. When the Office is of a solemn Octave or above, only one Prayer is said, unless a Commemoration must be made. Through a simple Octave and on an Octave day of three Lessons, several Prayers are said, as set out in title X.

III De DominicaOf the Sunday

1. Per totum annum, nisi festum, de quo in Dominicis sit agendum, impediat, et praeterquam in Vigiliis Natalis Domini, et Epiphaniae, occurrentibus in Dominicis, semper est Missa de Dominica celebranda.

1. Throughout the year the Mass of the Sunday is always to be celebrated, unless a feast which is to be kept on Sundays impedes it, and except when the Vigils of Christmas and of the Epiphany fall on a Sunday.

2. Dicuntur autem tres Orationes, ut hic infra tit. X praescribitur, exceptis Dominicis Palmarum et in Albis, in quibus una sola dicitur, et exceptis casibus, de quibus in tit. VIII.

2. Three Prayers are said, as prescribed below in title X, except on Palm Sunday and Low Sunday, when only one is said, and except in the cases given in title VIII.

3. Quando vero ob dicta impedimenta Missa Dominicae proprio die celebrari non potest, semper est dicenda (nisi ubi aliter notatur in propriis locis) infra hebdomadam propriam in aliqua Feria vacante, in qua legitur Homilia Dominicae: tunc autem celebratur juxta ritum Officii, de quo illo die fit, cum memoria, vel memoriis convenientibus.

3. When, because of these impediments, the Sunday Mass cannot be celebrated on its own day, it is always to be said (unless otherwise noted in its proper place) within its own week on a free Feria on which the Homily of the Sunday is read; it is then celebrated according to the rite of the Office said that day, with the fitting Commemoration or Commemorations.

4. In hujusmodi porro resumptionis die, occurrente festo Toto Duplici, Duplici, Semiduplici, vel Simplici, duae dicuntur Missae conventuales, una in Choro, altera extra Chorum ut infra tit. IV n. 2 dicetur; in Festis vero et diebus Octavis trium Lectionum et infra Octavas, et in Feriis una tantum Missa conventualis de ipsa Dominica. Missae autem privatae dici possunt aut de Officio diei cum Memoria Dominicae aut de ipsa Dominica cum Memoria Officii diei; additis in utraque Missa aliis Memoriis forte occurrentibus. Idem fiat (etiam omissa Homilia) de Missa alicujus Dominicae, quae post Oct. Epiphaniae vel Trinitatis resumi nequit, cujus tamen Evangelium legitur in fine omnium Missarum, in quibus de tali Dominica facta fuerit Memoria. — Si in Dominica IV Adventus evenerit Vigilia Nativitatis Domini, in Choro dicitur Missa Vigiliae, extra Chorum Missa de Dominica proprio ritu. Item Missa Dominicae IV post Oct. Paschae impeditae ipsa die Dominica dicitur extra Chorum. — Similiter Missa Dominicae, cujus Homilia infra hebdomadam legi nequit, dicitur ipsa die Dominica extra Chorum. — Si eodem die infra hebdomadam occurrant Missa Dominicae et Vigiliae, dicitur in Choro Missa Dominicae cum Memoria Vigiliae.

4. On such a day of resumption, if a feast Totum Duplex, Duplex, Semiduplex or Simplex occurs, two conventual Masses are said, one in Choir and the other outside Choir, as will be said below in title IV, n. 2; but on feasts and Octave days of three Lessons, within Octaves, and on Ferias, only one conventual Mass, of the Sunday itself. Private Masses may be said either of the Office of the day with a Commemoration of the Sunday, or of the Sunday with a Commemoration of the Office of the day, adding in either Mass any other Commemorations that occur. The same is done (even though the Homily is omitted) with the Mass of a Sunday which cannot be resumed after the Octave of the Epiphany or after Trinity; its Gospel, however, is read at the end of all the Masses in which that Sunday has been commemorated. — If the Vigil of Christmas falls on the fourth Sunday of Advent, the Mass of the Vigil is said in Choir, and outside Choir the Mass of the Sunday with its own rite. Likewise the Mass of the fourth Sunday after the Octave of Easter, when impeded, is said on the Sunday itself outside Choir. — Similarly the Mass of a Sunday whose Homily cannot be read during the week is said on the Sunday itself outside Choir. — If on the same weekday the Mass of a Sunday and that of a Vigil occur, the Mass of the Sunday is said in Choir with a Commemoration of the Vigil.

IV De Feria et VigiliaOf the Feria and the Vigil

1. Missa de Feria celebratur, quando non occurrit festum, vel Octava simplex et supra, vel non fit Missa pro mortuis. In Feriis, quae propriam non habent, dicitur Missa Dominicae praecedentis; vel dici possunt Missae votivae juxta ordinem Feriarum de Sancto vel Officio in Ordine nostro privilegiato, vel pro Missis privatis etiam aliae votivae, de quibus in sequenti titulo V agitur.

1. The Mass of the Feria is celebrated when no feast or Octave, simple or above, occurs, and no Mass for the dead is said. On Ferias which have no Mass of their own, the Mass of the preceding Sunday is said; or the votive Masses may be said, according to the order of the Ferias, of the Saint or Office privileged in our Order, or, for private Masses, other votive Masses as well, which are treated in title V below.

2. In omnibus Feriis Quadragesimae post diem Cinerum ac Passionis ante Dominicam Palmarum, in Feriis Quatuor Temporum extra Octavam Pentecostes, et in Vigiliis communibus quae propriam Missam habent, si occurrat Officium Totum Duplex, Duplex, Semiduplex vel Simplex, in Ecclesiis in quibus adest obligatio Chori, duae dicuntur Missae conventuales: una de Officio diei, sine Memoria Feriae aut Vigiliae, sed cum aliis Memoriis forte occurrentibus; altera de Feria aut Vigilia, sine Memoria Officii diei. Harum tamen una tantum dicitur in Choro cum assistentia Choralium; altera vero, sine hujusmodi assistentia Choralium, legitur extra Chorum. Missa porro in Choro dicenda cum assistentia Choralium, in Totis Duplicibus I et II classis et per Octavas privilegiatas Epiphaniae et SS. Corporis Christi, est Missa de Officio currenti; in reliquis Totis Duplicibus, Duplicibus, Semiduplicibus, et Simplicibus, Missa de Feria aut Vigilia. In Totis Duplicibus tamen I classis nihil umquam fit de Vigilia non privilegiata (excepta Vigilia Ascensionis, quae est feria major), juxta Rubricas. De die item infra Octavam non privilegiatam, si in Feriis aut Vigiliis supra recensitis occurrat, non dicitur Missa conventualis, sed fit tantum Memoria in Missa de Feria vel Vigilia. Missae autem privatae omnibus his diebus ad libitum Celebrantium dici possunt aut de Feria vel Vigilia cum Memoria Officii diei; aut de Officio diei cum Memoria Feriae vel Vigiliae et ultimo Evangelio Feriae. Si tamen Officium occurrens sit Totum Duplex I aut II classis, aut Octava privilegiata Epiphaniae et SS. Corporis Christi, Missa privata de Feria vel Vigilia non permittitur.

2. On all the Ferias of Lent after Ash Wednesday and of Passiontide before Palm Sunday, on Ember days outside the Octave of Pentecost, and on common Vigils that have a Mass of their own, if an Office Totum Duplex, Duplex, Semiduplex or Simplex occurs, two conventual Masses are said in churches bound to Choir: one of the Office of the day, without a Commemoration of the Feria or Vigil but with any other Commemorations that occur; the other of the Feria or Vigil, without a Commemoration of the Office of the day. Only one of them, however, is said in Choir with the choir assisting; the other is read outside Choir without the choir. The Mass said in Choir with the choir assisting is, on feasts Totum Duplex of the I and II class and through the privileged Octaves of the Epiphany and of Corpus Christi, the Mass of the current Office; on the other feasts Totum Duplex, Duplex, Semiduplex and Simplex, the Mass of the Feria or Vigil. On feasts Totum Duplex of the I class, however, nothing at all is done of a non-privileged Vigil (except the Vigil of the Ascension, which is a greater feria), according to the Rubrics. Of a day within a non-privileged Octave falling on the Ferias or Vigils listed above, no conventual Mass is said; it is only commemorated in the Mass of the Feria or Vigil. Private Masses on all these days may be said, as the celebrant chooses, either of the Feria or Vigil with a Commemoration of the Office of the day, or of the Office of the day with a Commemoration of the Feria or Vigil and the Gospel of the Feria at the end. If, however, the occurring Office is Totum Duplex of the I or II class, or the privileged Octave of the Epiphany or of Corpus Christi, a private Mass of the Feria or Vigil is not allowed.

3. Si qua ex dictis Feriis et Vigilia simul occurrant, Missa conventualis dicitur de Feria cum Memoria Vigiliae; et Missae privatae dici possunt aut de Feria aut de Vigilia cum Memoria alterius. Aut si fiat Officium Totius Duplicis, vel Duplicis, vel Octavae privilegiatae, vel Semiduplicis seu Simplicis, duae dicuntur Missae conventuales ut supra: una de Officio diei, sine Memoria Feriae et Vigiliae, sed cum Memoriis forte occurrentibus; altera de Feria, cum Memoria Vigiliae; et Missae privatae dici possunt aut de Feria, aut de Vigilia, aut de Officio diei cum Memoria de eis de quibus non dicitur Missa et ultimo Evangelio Feriae.

3. If one of these Ferias and a Vigil occur together, the conventual Mass is of the Feria with a Commemoration of the Vigil, and private Masses may be either of the Feria or of the Vigil with a Commemoration of the other. Or, if the Office is of a feast Totum Duplex, Duplex, a privileged Octave, Semiduplex or Simplex, two conventual Masses are said as above: one of the Office of the day, without a Commemoration of the Feria and Vigil but with any Commemorations that occur; the other of the Feria with a Commemoration of the Vigil. Private Masses may then be of the Feria, of the Vigil, or of the Office of the day, with a Commemoration of those whose Mass is not said and the Gospel of the Feria at the end.

4. Item die 25 Aprilis, vel, si die 25 Aprilis Pascha fuerit, Feria III post Pascha, et in diebus Rogationum, si occurrat Officium Totius Duplicis, Duplicis, Octavae privilegiatae, Semiduplicis vel Simplicis (sive Processio fiat, sive non), duae dicuntur Missae conventuales ut supra n. 2 dictum est: una de Rogationibus (vel de Vigilia Ascensionis cum Memoria Rogationum), altera vero de Officio diei. In festis tamen Totis Duplicibus I et II classis aut infra Octavam Paschae, si fiat Processio, in Choro dicitur tum Missa de Officio diei tum Missa de Rogationibus; si vero Processio non fiat, dicitur unica Missa conventualis de die cum memoria Feriae (vel Vigiliae) et ultimo Evangelio Rogationum. In omnibus Litaniarum diebus, dummodo Officium de Feria dictum non fuerit, Missae privatae non permittuntur de Rogationibus, sed Memoria Feriae (vel Vigiliae) et ultimum Evangelium Rogationum dicitur in omnibus Missis privatis, quae non sunt Defunctorum.

4. Likewise on 25 April (or, if 25 April is Easter Day, on the Tuesday after Easter) and on the Rogation days, if an Office Totum Duplex, Duplex, of a privileged Octave, Semiduplex or Simplex occurs (whether or not the Procession is held), two conventual Masses are said, as said above in n. 2: one of the Rogations (or of the Vigil of the Ascension with a Commemoration of the Rogations), the other of the Office of the day. On feasts Totum Duplex of the I and II class, however, or within the Octave of Easter, if the Procession is held, both the Mass of the Office of the day and the Mass of the Rogations are said in Choir; if it is not held, a single conventual Mass is said, of the day, with a Commemoration of the Feria (or Vigil) and the Gospel of the Rogations at the end. On all the days of the Litanies, unless the Office was of the Feria, private Masses of the Rogations are not allowed; instead the Commemoration of the Feria (or Vigil) and the last Gospel of the Rogations are said in all private Masses that are not Masses for the Dead.

5. Propter paucitatem Fratrum in praedictis Feriis vel Vigiliis occurrentibus cum festo, una tantum Missa conventualis celebretur, prout dicitur in sequenti n. 6.

5. Where the brethren are few, only one conventual Mass is celebrated on these Ferias or Vigils when they fall with a feast, as is said in n. 6 following.

6. In Ecclesiis autem in quibus una tantum habeatur Missa conventualis, quoties occurrat aliqua ex Feriis vel Vigiliis n. 2 et 3 recensitis, et si non fiat Processio in diebus Rogationum, ipsa Missa conventualis in Totis Duplicibus I et II classis et per Octavas privilegiatas Epiphaniae et SS. Corporis Christi dicitur de Officio diei cum Memoria et ultimo Evangelio Feriae, et extra Tota Duplicia I classis, etiam cum Memoria Vigiliae. In Totis Duplicibus vero, et Duplicibus, Semiduplicibus et Simplicibus, eadem Missa dicitur de Feria aut Vigilia cum Memoria Officii diei; et, si Feria et Vigilia simul occurrant, de Feria cum Memoria Officii diei ac Vigiliae et Memoriis occurrentibus (vel in Vigilia Ascensionis, de ipsa Vigilia cum Memoria Rogationum). Item in diebus Litaniarum si fiat Processio et in Ecclesia habeatur unica Missa, ea dicitur de Rogationibus (vel Vigilia Ascensionis) cum Memoria Officii diei; nisi hoc fuerit Totum Duplex I classis, quo in casu dicitur Missa de Festo cum Memoria Feriae (vel Vigiliae) et ultimo Evangelio Rogationum.

6. In churches that have only one conventual Mass, whenever one of the Ferias or Vigils listed in nn. 2 and 3 occurs, and when the Procession is not held on the Rogation days, that conventual Mass is said, on feasts Totum Duplex of the I and II class and through the privileged Octaves of the Epiphany and of Corpus Christi, of the Office of the day with a Commemoration of the Feria and its Gospel at the end, and, outside feasts Totum Duplex of the I class, with a Commemoration of the Vigil as well. On the other feasts Totum Duplex, and on Duplex, Semiduplex and Simplex feasts, the same Mass is said of the Feria or Vigil with a Commemoration of the Office of the day; and if a Feria and a Vigil occur together, of the Feria, with Commemorations of the Office of the day, of the Vigil and of any others that occur (or, on the Vigil of the Ascension, of the Vigil itself with a Commemoration of the Rogations). Likewise on the days of the Litanies, if the Procession is held and the church has only one Mass, it is said of the Rogations (or of the Vigil of the Ascension) with a Commemoration of the Office of the day; unless that is a feast Totum Duplex of the I class, in which case the Mass of the feast is said with a Commemoration of the Feria (or Vigil) and the Gospel of the Rogations at the end.

V De Missis votivisOf Votive Masses

1. In Feriis minoribus, in quibus nec Missa Dominicae resumenda, nec Missa pro defunctis celebranda est, loco Missae Dominicae praecedentis, dici potest Missa conventualis votiva juxta Ordinem Feriarum de Sancto vel Officio in Ordine nostro olim privilegiato, idest: Feria III de S. P. N. Dominico, Feria IV de S. Thoma Aquinate, et Feria V de SS. Sacramento, aut de D. N. J. C. Summo et Aeterno Sacerdote. Hae Missae votivae, excepta Missa de D. N. J. C. Sacerdote, dicuntur extra Septuagesimam cum Gloria et tria Alleluia post Epistolam vel ℟., vel infra hoc tempus cum Tractu. Feria III dicitur etiam Sequentia. Feria VI dici potest Missa de sancta Cruce, vel pro Fratribus mortuis illo anno, vel de S. Vincentio (semel in mense) vel alio, juxta consuetudinem Provinciarum, sed feriali ritu. Sabbato dicitur Missa votiva de B. Maria V. pro tempore, si Officium votivum de ipsa actum fuerit.

1. On lesser Ferias, when neither the Mass of a Sunday is to be resumed nor a Mass for the dead to be celebrated, instead of the Mass of the preceding Sunday a votive conventual Mass may be said, according to the order of the Ferias, of the Saint or Office formerly privileged in our Order: on Tuesday of our holy Father Dominic, on Wednesday of St Thomas Aquinas, and on Thursday of the Blessed Sacrament or of our Lord Jesus Christ the High and Eternal Priest. These votive Masses, except that of our Lord Jesus Christ the Priest, are said outside Septuagesima with the Gloria and three Alleluias after the Epistle or Responsory, and within that time with the Tract. On Tuesday the Sequence is also said. On Friday the Mass of the Holy Cross may be said, or for the brethren who have died that year, or of St Vincent (once a month), or another, according to the custom of the Provinces, but with the ferial rite. On Saturday the votive Mass of Our Lady proper to the season is said, if her votive Office has been said.

2. Missae votivae privatae sine cantu prohibentur in omnibus Officiis Totis Duplicibus, et Duplicibus, in Dominicis, licet resumptis vel quoad Missam translatis, per Octavas privilegiatas, in Feriis Quatuor Temporum, in Feriis Adventus a die 17 ad 23 Decembris inclusive occurrentibus, in Feriis Quadragesimae a die Cinerum et Passionis ad Feriam IV Majoris Hebdomadae inclusive, diebus Rogationum, in Vigiliis, et in diebus octavis solemnibus, simplicibus et trium Lectionum, etiam si de iis Memoria tantum facta fuerit in Officio; proinde legi tantum possunt, ex rationabili tamen causa, in Festis Semiduplicibus, Simplicibus, diebus infra Octavas non privilegiatas, Feria VI post Octavam Ascensionis, Festis trium Lectionum et Feriis superius non exceptis.

2. Private votive Masses without chant are forbidden on all Offices Totum Duplex and Duplex; on Sundays, even when resumed or transferred as to the Mass; through privileged Octaves; on Ember days; on the Ferias of Advent from 17 to 23 December inclusive; on the Ferias of Lent from Ash Wednesday, and of Passiontide, to Wednesday of Holy Week inclusive; on the Rogation days; on Vigils; and on Octave days, solemn, simple and of three Lessons, even if they have only been commemorated in the Office. They may therefore be read only, and for a reasonable cause, on Semiduplex and Simplex feasts, on days within non-privileged Octaves, on the Friday after the Octave of the Ascension, on feasts of three Lessons, and on the Ferias not excepted above.

3. Missa votiva pro Sponsis, cum propria benedictione, permittitur quotidie per annum extra tempus clausum; et etiam tempore clauso, quoties Ordinarius loci ex justa causa benedictionem nuptialem permiserit. Excipiuntur Dominicae, Festa de praecepto, licet suppressa, Tota Duplicia I et II classis, Octavae privilegiatae (non tamen Nativitatis, Ascensionis Domini et SS. Cordis Jesu), Feriae ac Vigiliae privilegiatae. In his autem casibus exceptis dicitur Missa de die, in eaque additur Oratio pro Sponsis sub unica conclusione cum prima, atque datur benedictio eis propria. Attamen in die Commemorationis Omnium Fidelium Defunctorum prohibentur Missa et Oratio, necnon benedictio. Item haec benedictio numquam dari potest extra Missam, si tum Missa pro Sponsis, tum ejus Memoria, ideoque benedictio locum habere nequeat, si Sponsi non sint praesentes, vel si uterque aut alteruter alias benedictionem acceperit: servata nihilominus, sicubi vigeat, consuetudine benedictionis iis, ut supra, impertiendae, si hanc vir tantum obtinuerit. Quod si nuptiae tempore clauso contractae fuerint, non concessa a loci Ordinario venia benedictionis nuptiarum, Missa cum propria benedictione differtur ad diem in qua, extra tempus clausum, Missa pro Sponsis vel ejus Memoria permittitur.

3. The votive Mass for Bride and Bridegroom, with its own blessing, is allowed every day of the year outside the closed time, and in the closed time too whenever the local Ordinary has allowed the nuptial blessing for a just cause. Excepted are Sundays, feasts of obligation (even if suppressed), feasts Totum Duplex of the I and II class, privileged Octaves (but not those of Christmas, the Ascension and the Sacred Heart), and privileged Ferias and Vigils. In these excepted cases the Mass of the day is said, the Prayer for the Bride and Bridegroom is added under one conclusion with the first, and their own blessing is given. On the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed, however, the Mass, the Prayer and the blessing are all forbidden. This blessing can never be given outside Mass; and where neither the Mass for the Bride and Bridegroom nor its Commemoration is possible, the blessing cannot take place either; nor if the couple are not present, or if both or either have received the blessing before (keeping, however, wherever it exists, the custom of giving it as above if only the husband has received it). If the marriage was contracted in the closed time without the local Ordinary's leave for the nuptial blessing, the Mass with its own blessing is deferred to a day outside the closed time on which the Mass for Bride and Bridegroom, or its Commemoration, is allowed.

4. Missa votiva solemnis pro re gravi et publica simul causa, quae scilicet de mandato vel consensu Ordinarii loci pro qualibet vice obtinendo in cantu celebratur cum concursu populi, permittitur singulis diebus, extra Dominicas I classis, Vigilias Nativitatis Domini et Pentecostes, Ferias privilegiatas, Tota Duplicia I classis et Commemorationem Omnium Fidelium Defunctorum. Haec tamen Missa cani nequit die in qua fiat Officium aut Memoria, vel occurrat Vigilia aut dies infra Octavam, quamvis solius Memoriae, de identico Domini Mysterio, vel de eadem Sancti persona, de qua esset canenda Missa; tunc enim, loco Missae votivae, canitur Missa Officio vel Memoriae, Vigiliae aut Octavae conveniens, cum Memoriis quae in ipsa Missa votiva faciendae essent, ut infra tit. VIII, n. 3, habetur. Aliis vero diebus in quibus hujusmodi Missa ut supra, vel ut infra n. 9 impediatur, sed ejus Memoria juxta eumdem tit. VIII, n. 3 non vetetur (vetatur autem haec Memoria in Commemoratione Omnium Fidelium Defunctorum et in Toto Duplici I classis Domini primario universalis Ecclesiae, praeterquam Feria II et III Paschatis et Pentecostes, in quibus eadem Memoria permittitur), in Missa cantata diei occurrentis, etsi conventuali, sub unica conclusione additur Oratio de Missa votiva impedita, et fiunt tantummodo Memoriae quae huic Missae conveniunt. Inter Missas votivas solemnes computanda est Missa de Spiritu Sancto a Constitutionibus praescripta in Electione Reverendissimi Magistri Generalis et Prioris Provincialis vel Conventualis. In Vigilia tamen Pentecostes pro electione Reverendissimi Magistri Generalis cantanda est Missa diei cum Introitu Cum sanctificatus fuero, una Oratione, Gloria et Credo.

4. A solemn votive Mass for a grave and at the same time public cause, sung with the people assembled by order or with the consent of the local Ordinary, obtained each time, is allowed on every day except Sundays of the I class, the Vigils of Christmas and Pentecost, privileged Ferias, feasts Totum Duplex of the I class and the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed. This Mass cannot be sung, however, on a day which has an Office or a Commemoration, or a Vigil or a day within an Octave (even one only commemorated), of the same Mystery of our Lord or the same Saint as the Mass to be sung; for then, instead of the votive Mass, the Mass proper to that Office, Commemoration, Vigil or Octave is sung, with the Commemorations that would have been made in the votive Mass, as in title VIII, n. 3 below. On other days on which such a Mass is impeded, as above or as in n. 9 below, but its Commemoration is not forbidden by that same title VIII, n. 3 (it is forbidden on the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed and on a primary feast of our Lord Totum Duplex of the I class of the universal Church, except Easter and Whit Monday and Tuesday, when it is allowed), the Prayer of the impeded votive Mass is added under one conclusion in the sung Mass of the day, even the conventual Mass, and only the Commemorations proper to this Mass are made. Among the solemn votive Masses is counted the Mass of the Holy Ghost prescribed by the Constitutions at the election of the Most Reverend Master General and of a Prior Provincial or Conventual. On the Vigil of Pentecost, however, for the election of the Master General, the Mass of the day is sung with the Introit Cum sanctificatus fuero, one Prayer, the Gloria and the Credo.

5. In anniversariis electionis et coronationis Summi Pontificis in omnibus Missis, juxta normas numero proxime sequenti statuendas, additur Oratio pro Papa.

5. On the anniversaries of the election and coronation of the Supreme Pontiff the Prayer for the Pope is added in all Masses, according to the norms laid down in the next number.

6. In anniversario autem electionis vel translationis et consecrationis Episcopi, in omnibus Ecclesiis etiam Regularium, dummodo Memoria Missae votivae juxta num. 4 non prohibeatur, ad omnes Missas conventuales et privatas, praeterquam Defunctorum, ultimo loco post Orationes a Rubricis praescriptas additur Oratio pro Episcopo.

6. On the anniversary of the election or translation and of the consecration of the Bishop, in all churches, those of Regulars too, provided the Commemoration of a votive Mass is not forbidden under n. 4, the Prayer for the Bishop is added in all conventual and private Masses, except Masses for the Dead, in the last place after the Prayers prescribed by the Rubrics.

7. Quod si hujusmodi anniversaria Summi Pontificis pro tota Ecclesia, vel Episcopi pro tota Dioecesi respective, in perpetuum impediantur, Memoria eorumdem fiat die, in quam transferantur.

7. If these anniversaries, of the Supreme Pontiff for the whole Church or of the Bishop for the whole diocese, are perpetually impeded, their Commemoration is made on the day to which they are transferred.

8. Missae votivae in cantu, quamvis non sint pro re gravi et publica simul causa, permittuntur singulis diebus liberis ab Officio Toto Duplici, Duplici, a Dominicis quibusvis licet resumptis vel quoad Missam translatis, et ab aliqua ex Feriis, Vigiliis aut Octavis privilegiatis.

8. Sung votive Masses, even when not for a grave and public cause, are allowed on every day free of an Office Totum Duplex or Duplex, of any Sunday (even resumed, or transferred as to the Mass), and of a privileged Feria, Vigil or Octave.

9. Attamen Missae omnes superius indicatae, sicut et Missae inferius tit. VII, n. 1-5 recensendae, quamvis eas ritus diei currentis alioquin permitteret, prohibentur in Ecclesiis ubi cujuslibet Missae conventualis onus urgeat, cui per alios Sacerdotes satisfieri nequeat; et in Ecclesiis unam tantum Missam habentibus, prohibentur etiam die 2 Februarii, si fiat benedictio Candelarum, et in Litaniis, si fiat Processio.

9. All the Masses given above, and those to be listed below in title VII, nn. 1-5, even if the rite of the day would otherwise allow them, are forbidden in churches where the obligation of any conventual Mass presses which cannot be met by other priests; and in churches that have only one Mass they are also forbidden on 2 February, if the Candles are blessed, and on the Litanies, if the Procession is held.

VI De Missis DefunctorumOf Masses for the Dead

1. In Commemoratione Omnium Fidelium Defunctorum tres Missae pro Defunctis permittuntur.

1. On the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed three Masses for the Dead are allowed.

2. Prima die cujusque hebdomadae non impedita Festo trium Lectionum et supra, vel Octava quacumque vel Feria aut Vigilia habentibus propriam Missam, vel a resumenda Missa Dominicae impeditae, semper loco Missae conventualis de die celebranda est Missa pro Defunctis, id est de Requiem. Quod si Missa de Requiem ob continuas Festivitates vel dies supra relatos infra hebdomadam celebrari non possit, tunc alia hebdomadae die, minus solemni, in Missa conventuali, quae nihilominus ipsis Defunctis in Conventibus I Ordinis applicetur, additur Oratio Omnipotens pro Vivis et Defunctis post Orationes a Rubricis praescriptas. Haec tamen Missa omittatur infra hebdomadam in qua Omnium Fidelium Defunctorum Commemorationem universa celebrat Ecclesia, et infra hebdomadam Paschae et Pentecostes, ac infra hebdomadam Sanctam et illam in qua incidit Nativitas D. N. J. C., et quando infra hebdomadam jam pro Fratre defuncto exsequiae factae fuerint, et denique infra hebdomadas, in quibus celebrantur quatuor Ordinis Anniversaria in Kalendario notata. Haec quatuor Anniversaria cantari possunt in Festis Totis Duplicibus communibus et infra, non tamen in Festis de praecepto licet suppressis, Dominicis, Feriis, Vigiliis Octavisque privilegiatis, neque in Festis I et II classis etiam translatis; quo in casu transferantur in diem sequentem similiter non impeditam. Illis autem diebus, quibus cantatur Missa in hujusmodi quatuor Anniversariis, non omittatur Missa conventualis de die in Choro dicenda, praeterquam diebus, in quibus juxta n. 2 unica Missa conventualis de Requiem sufficit. Ubi autem duae Missae conventuales nequeunt celebrari, unica Missa de Festo applicatur pro Defunctis et additur post Orationes a Rubricis praescriptas Oratio Omnipotens pro Vivis et Defunctis.

2. On the first day of each week not impeded by a feast of three Lessons or above, by any Octave, by a Feria or Vigil with a Mass of its own, or by the resumption of an impeded Sunday Mass, the Mass for the Dead, that is, of Requiem, is always celebrated instead of the conventual Mass of the day. If the Requiem Mass cannot be celebrated within the week because of continuous feasts or the days just mentioned, then on another, less solemn day of the week the Prayer Omnipotens for the Living and the Dead is added, after the Prayers prescribed by the Rubrics, in the conventual Mass, which in convents of the First Order is nevertheless applied for the dead. This Mass is omitted, however, in the week in which the universal Church keeps the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed, in Easter week and Whitsun week, in Holy Week and the week in which the Nativity of our Lord falls, when the funeral of a brother has already been held during the week, and lastly in the weeks in which the four Anniversaries of the Order noted in the Calendar are kept. These four Anniversaries may be sung on common feasts Totum Duplex and below, but not on feasts of obligation (even suppressed), Sundays, privileged Ferias, Vigils and Octaves, nor on feasts of the I and II class, even transferred; in that case they are transferred to the next day that is likewise not impeded. On the days on which the Mass of these four Anniversaries is sung, the conventual Mass of the day to be said in Choir is not omitted, except on the days on which, according to n. 2, a single conventual Mass of Requiem suffices. Where two conventual Masses cannot be celebrated, the single Mass of the feast is applied for the dead and the Prayer Omnipotens for the Living and the Dead is added after the Prayers prescribed by the Rubrics.

3. In Ecclesia in qua agitur exsequiale funus alicujus Defuncti, etiam absente rationabilem ob causam vel jam sepulto cadavere, permittitur unica Missa cantata vel etiam, pro pauperibus, lecta pro die obitus. Haec vero prohibetur in Totis Duplicibus I classis primariis universalis Ecclesiae, exceptis Feriis II et III post Pascha et Pentecosten in quibus permittitur, in Festis Dedicationis ac Tituli Ecclesiae ipsius in qua funus agi deberet, necnon Patroni principalis loci, et pro Religiosis Institutis, in Festis Tituli aut Sancti Fundatoris Ordinis seu Congregationis; dummodo haec Festa non fuerint eo anno quoad solemnitatem translata, quo in casu prohibetur in Dominicis in quibus agitur solemnitas externa Festorum hujusmodi. Quoties autem a Rubricis, ut supra, haec Missa impediatur, transferri potest in proximiorem sequentem diem, similiter non impeditam. Quod si in die Commemorationis Fidelium Defunctorum agatur funus alicujus Defuncti, sumatur prima Missa, et in Ecclesiis et Oratoriis ubi prima Missa solemniter celebrata fuerit, aut pro Officio diei distincte sit dein celebranda, dicatur secunda, aut demum tertia Missa; addita tamen in ipsa Missa exsequiali, sub unica conclusione, Oratione pro eodem Defuncto, ut pro die obitus.

3. In the church in which the funeral of someone deceased is held, even if the body is absent for a reasonable cause or already buried, one sung Mass for the day of death is allowed, or, for the poor, a read one. It is forbidden, however, on the primary feasts Totum Duplex of the I class of the universal Church (except Easter and Whit Monday and Tuesday, when it is allowed), on the feasts of the Dedication and Title of the very church in which the funeral is to be held, on that of the principal Patron of the place, and, for Religious Institutes, on the feasts of the Title or holy Founder of the Order or Congregation; provided these feasts have not been transferred that year as to their solemnity, in which case it is forbidden on the Sundays on which the external solemnity of such feasts is kept. Whenever this Mass is impeded by the Rubrics as above, it may be transferred to the nearest following day that is likewise not impeded. If the funeral is held on the Commemoration of the Faithful Departed, the first Mass is taken; and in churches and oratories where the first Mass has been celebrated solemnly, or is still to be celebrated separately for the Office of the day, the second or else the third Mass is said; but in that funeral Mass the Prayer for the same deceased, as for the day of death, is added under one conclusion.

4. Item in Ecclesia, aut Oratorio publico, ubi funus Defuncti solemniter agitur, dummodo sacrificium pro ipso Defuncto applicetur, ipsa die dici possunt Missae privatae de Requiem pro die obitus, ut supra, nisi occurrat Officium Missas Defunctorum sequenti numero indicatas impediens. Missae hujusmodi privatae pariter celebrari possunt unica die ad libitum, ab obitu usque ad depositionem Defuncti, in Oratoriis semipublicis, quae, deficiente Ecclesia vel Oratorio publico, horum locum teneant, necnon quotidie per idem tempus in Oratoriis semipublicis, quae locum non teneant Ecclesiae aut Oratorii publici, et in Oratoriis stricte privatis domus Defuncti, dummodo cadaver sit physice praesens in domo, ubi erecta sunt ipsa Oratoria, et dies impedita non occurrat.

4. Likewise, in a church or public oratory where the funeral of the deceased is held solemnly, provided the Sacrifice is applied for that person, private Requiem Masses for the day of death may be said on that same day, as above, unless an Office occurs which impedes the Masses for the Dead indicated in the next number. Such private Masses may also be celebrated on one day of choice, between the death and the burial, in semi-public oratories which take the place of a church or public oratory where there is none; and daily during that time in semi-public oratories which do not take the place of a church or public oratory, and in the strictly private oratories of the house of the deceased, provided the body is physically present in the house where those oratories are, and an impeded day does not occur.

5. In die autem III, VII, XXX et anniversaria ab obitu vel depositione Defunctorum, et opportuniori die post acceptum mortis nuntium, in qualibet Ecclesia permittitur unica Missa pro Defuncto, ut in die obitus, cantata vel etiam lecta; dummodo non occurrat Dominica, aut Festum de praecepto, licet suppressum, Commemoratio Omnium Fidelium Defunctorum, Totum Duplex I vel II classis, etiam translatum, aut aliqua ex Feriis, Vigiliis vel Octavis privilegiatis, quo in casu, hujusmodi Missa, in proximiorem diem, pariter non impeditam anticipari, si anticipari valeat, aut transferri potest, dummodo in cantu celebretur.

5. On the third, seventh and thirtieth day and the anniversary from the death or burial of the deceased, and on a convenient day after news of the death has been received, one Mass for the deceased, as on the day of death, sung or even read, is allowed in any church; provided there does not occur a Sunday, a feast of obligation (even suppressed), the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed, a feast Totum Duplex of the I or II class (even transferred), or a privileged Feria, Vigil or Octave. In that case such a Mass may be anticipated, if it can be, or transferred, to the nearest day likewise not impeded, provided it is sung.

6. Idem servatur pro unica Missa cantata in Anniversariis quae extra diem obitus ex fundatione celebrentur, vel quae pro omnibus Defunctis alicujus coetus semel quolibet anno habentur. Idem quoque servatur pro Missis quae per octiduum, a Commemoratione Omnium Fidelium Defunctorum inclusive computandum, pro fidelium pietate pariter in cantu peraguntur; quae tamen Missae extra octiduum ipsum anticipari et transferri nequeunt.

6. The same holds for a single sung Mass on Anniversaries celebrated by foundation on a day other than that of death, or held once each year for all the dead of some association. The same holds too for Masses sung for the devotion of the faithful during the eight days counted from the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed inclusive; these Masses, however, cannot be anticipated or transferred outside those eight days.

7. Item, in Ecclesia vel Oratorio publico ac principali sepulcreti, immo et in quolibet Sacello sepulcreti rite erecto vel erigendo, Missae quae inibi celebrari permittuntur, possunt esse de Requiem; dummodo non occurrat Dominica, aut Festum de praecepto, licet suppressum, Totum Duplex I vel II classis, etiam translatum, aut aliqua ex Feriis, Vigiliis vel Octavis privilegiatis. Hoc tamen privilegium non favet aliis Ecclesiis vel Capellis extra coemeterium, subter quas, licet ad legitimam distantiam, alicujus Defuncti cadaver quiescit; neque Ecclesiis, Oratoriis publicis et Sacellis sepulcreti, in quo olim cadavera sepeliebantur, quod sepulcretum tamen qualibet ex causa derelictum est, ita ut Defuncti sepeliri in eo non amplius soleant; neque Ecclesiis, quae licet circumjacens habeant coemeterium, tamen adjunctum chorale onus, aut curam animarum adnexam habent.

7. Likewise, in the church or principal public oratory of a cemetery, and indeed in any chapel of a cemetery rightly erected or to be erected, the Masses allowed to be celebrated there may be of Requiem; provided there does not occur a Sunday, a feast of obligation (even suppressed), a feast Totum Duplex of the I or II class (even transferred), or a privileged Feria, Vigil or Octave. This privilege does not extend to other churches or chapels outside the cemetery under which, even at the lawful distance, the body of someone deceased rests; nor to the churches, public oratories and chapels of a burial ground in which the dead were once buried but which has been abandoned for whatever cause, so that the dead are no longer usually buried there; nor to churches which, though they have a cemetery around them, have an obligation of Choir or the care of souls attached to them.

8. Missae quotidianae Defunctorum quae celebrantur in cantu, permittuntur singulis diebus in quibus non fiat Officium Totum Duplex vel Duplex, neque occurrat Dominica, licet resumpta vel quoad Missam translata, aut aliqua ex Feriis, Vigiliis vel Octavis privilegiatis. Missae vero quotidianae Defunctorum sine cantu, iis tantum permittuntur diebus in quibus fiat Festum Semiduplex, Simplex, de die infra Octavam solemnem et simplicem, de Feria VI post Octavam Ascensionis, de Feria majori Adventus, de sancta Maria in Sabbato, de Festo trium Lectionum et de Feria minori per annum; dummodo Octavae privilegiatae, Feriae Quatuor Temporum, Feriae Rogationum, Feriae Antiphonarum majorum a die 17 ad 23 Decembris inclusive, aliqua Vigilia, aut dies octava Solemnis, Simplex vel trium Lectionum non occurrant, nec primo resumenda sit Missa alicujus Dominicae impeditae. In Quadragesima, Missae quotidianae Defunctorum sine cantu permittuntur prima tantum die cujusque hebdomadae libera, in qua nempe, extra Quatuor Tempora et Vigilias, fiat de Festo Semiduplici vel Simplici aut de Feria non privilegiata, juxta Kalendarium Ecclesiae in qua Missa celebratur.

8. Daily Masses for the Dead that are sung are allowed on every day on which the Office is not Totum Duplex or Duplex, and there does not occur a Sunday (even resumed, or transferred as to the Mass) or a privileged Feria, Vigil or Octave. Daily Masses for the Dead without chant are allowed only on days whose Office is of a Semiduplex or Simplex feast, of a day within a solemn or simple Octave, of the Friday after the Octave of the Ascension, of a greater Feria of Advent, of Holy Mary on Saturday, of a feast of three Lessons, or of a lesser Feria of the year; provided there does not occur a privileged Octave, an Ember day, a Rogation day, a Feria of the great Antiphons from 17 to 23 December inclusive, a Vigil, or a solemn, simple or three-Lesson Octave day, and that the Mass of an impeded Sunday is not first to be resumed. In Lent, daily Masses for the Dead without chant are allowed only on the first free day of each week, that is, one on which, outside the Ember days and Vigils, the Office is of a Semiduplex or Simplex feast or of a non-privileged Feria, according to the Calendar of the church in which the Mass is celebrated.

9. Quaelibet tamen Missa Defunctorum, etiam in die aut pro die obitus, in Ecclesiis ubi habeatur expositio SS. Sacramenti, prohibetur toto expositionis tempore, praeterquam Missae de Commemoratione Omnium Fidelium Defunctorum. Prohibetur insuper quoties urgeat obligatio Missae cujuslibet conventualis vel parochialis, cui per alios Sacerdotes satisfieri nequeat; item in Ecclesiis unam tantum Missam habentibus prohibetur die 2 Februarii, Feria IV Cinerum, Dominica Palmarum et in Vigilia Pentecostes, si respective fiat benedictio Candelarum, Cinerum, Palmarum aut Fontis baptismalis, et in diebus Litaniarum, si fiat Processio.

9. Any Mass for the Dead, however, even on or for the day of death, is forbidden in churches where the Blessed Sacrament is exposed, for the whole time of the exposition, except the Masses of the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed. It is also forbidden whenever the obligation of any conventual or parochial Mass presses which cannot be met by other priests; and in churches that have only one Mass it is forbidden on 2 February, Ash Wednesday, Palm Sunday and the Vigil of Pentecost, if the blessing of the Candles, the Ashes, the Palms or the baptismal Font respectively takes place, and on the days of the Litanies, if the Procession is held.

VII De Occurrentia et de Translatione FestorumOf the Occurrence and Transfer of Feasts

1. Si Festum Patroni principalis, aut Tituli vel Dedicationis propriae Ecclesiae, aut etiam Tituli vel Sancti Fundatoris Ordinis seu Congregationis, per accidens impediatur, de eo in sua die permittitur una Missa in cantu, vel Memoria in Missa cantata diei currentis, quamvis conventuali, juxta ea quae de Missis votivis solemnibus tit. V, num. 4, habentur.

1. If the feast of the principal Patron, or of the Title or Dedication of the church itself, or of the Title or holy Founder of the Order or Congregation, is accidentally impeded, one sung Mass of it is allowed on its own day, or a Commemoration in the sung Mass of the day, even the conventual Mass, according to what is said of solemn votive Masses in title V, n. 4.

2. Idem etiam servatur in Ecclesiis, ubi cum magno populi concursu, cujus rei judex est loci Ordinarius, celebratur Festum quod transferri aut commemorari vel accidentaliter omitti debeat, aut de Mysterio, Sancto vel Beato, cujus mentio illa die fiat in Martyrologio, vel in ejus appendice pro respectivis Ecclesiis approbata. Hujusmodi tamen Missa dicenda est cum Memoriis, quae respondeant ritui Totius Duplicis I vel II classis, si sub hoc ritu Festum in proprio Kalendario fuerit inscriptum; secus cum omnibus Memoriis ritui Totius Duplicis communis et Duplicis congruentibus, juxta Rubricas.

2. The same is observed in churches where, with a great concourse of people (of which the local Ordinary is the judge), a feast is kept which has to be transferred, commemorated or accidentally omitted, or a Mystery, Saint or Blessed who is mentioned that day in the Martyrology or in its appendix approved for the church concerned. Such a Mass, however, is said with the Commemorations proper to the rite Totum Duplex of the I or II class, if the feast is entered with that rite in the church's own Calendar; otherwise with all the Commemorations befitting the rite of a common Totum Duplex and Duplex, according to the Rubrics.

3. In Dominicis minoribus per annum, in Ecclesiis et Oratoriis publicis aut semipublicis, ubi reponatur solemnitas externa Festi Patroni principalis, aut Tituli vel Dedicationis propriae Ecclesiae, aut etiam Tituli vel Sancti Fundatoris Ordinis seu Congregationis, quod infra praecedentem hebdomadam occurrerit, cani potest Missa de solemnitate translata, et de ea pariter legi potest unica Missa, nisi occurrat Totum Duplex I classis. Quod si solemnitas externa transferenda sit in Dominicam majorem, aut in Dominicam, ut supra, impeditam, Missae de solemnitate externa prohibentur; sed in Missa cantata diei currentis, etsi conventuali, et in altera lecta, fit Memoria de solemnitate externa, juxta normas pro Missis votivis solemnibus traditas tit. V, num. 4.

3. On the lesser Sundays of the year, in churches and in public or semi-public oratories where the external solemnity is kept of the feast of the principal Patron, of the Title or Dedication of the church itself, or of the Title or holy Founder of the Order or Congregation, which fell in the preceding week, the Mass of the transferred solemnity may be sung, and one read Mass of it is likewise allowed, unless a feast Totum Duplex of the I class occurs. If the external solemnity would have to be transferred to a greater Sunday, or to a Sunday impeded as above, Masses of the external solemnity are forbidden; but in the sung Mass of the day, even the conventual Mass, and in one other read Mass, a Commemoration of the external solemnity is made, according to the norms given for solemn votive Masses in title V, n. 4.

4. De Festis Totis Duplicibus communibus, Duplicibus aut Semiduplicibus et Simplicibus, quae perpetuo vel accidentaliter impediantur, dici possunt Missae etiam privatae ad libitum Sacerdotis; dummodo Officium impediens non fuerit Totum Duplex I vel II classis, Dominica quaevis, licet resumpta vel quoad Missam translata, Octava Epiphaniae, Paschae, Pentecostes et Corporis Christi, dies Octava Nativitatis et Ascensionis Domini, vel aliqua ex Feriis aut Vigiliis privilegiatis.

4. Of common feasts Totum Duplex, Duplex, Semiduplex and Simplex which are perpetually or accidentally impeded, Masses may be said, even private ones, as the priest chooses; provided the impeding Office is not Totum Duplex of the I or II class, any Sunday (even resumed, or transferred as to the Mass), the Octave of the Epiphany, of Easter, of Pentecost or of Corpus Christi, the Octave day of Christmas or of the Ascension, or a privileged Feria or Vigil.

5. Singulis diebus, in quibus non occurrat Officium Totum Duplex, Duplex, Dominica quaevis, licet resumpta vel quoad Missam translata, Octava privilegiata, Feria Quadragesimae a die Cinerum, Passionis, Quatuor Temporum et Rogationum, aut qualibet Vigilia, dici possunt, ritu festivo, Missae etiam privatae de quovis Officio ad Laudes commemorato aut de Mysterio, Sancto vel Beato, cujus mentio illa die fiat in Martyrologio, vel in ejus appendice pro respectivis Ecclesiis approbata; salvis amplioribus privilegiis quae in praesenti tit. VII, num. 1-4, ac tit. V, num. 8, sunt indicata.

5. On every day on which there does not occur an Office Totum Duplex or Duplex, any Sunday (even resumed, or transferred as to the Mass), a privileged Octave, a Feria of Lent from Ash Wednesday, of Passiontide, of the Ember days or the Rogations, or any Vigil, Masses may be said with the festal rite, even private ones, of any Office commemorated at Lauds, or of a Mystery, Saint or Blessed mentioned that day in the Martyrology or in its appendix approved for the church concerned; without prejudice to the wider privileges indicated in this title VII, nn. 1-4, and in title V, n. 8.

6. Omnes et singuli Sacerdotes, tam saeculares quam regulares, Missas, etsi Regularium proprias, omnino celebrent juxta Kalendarium Ecclesiae, vel Oratorii publici, in quo celebrant; exclusis tamen peculiaribus ritibus Ordinum et Ecclesiarum propriis. Idem servetur in Oratorio semipublico, sive in Capella principali Seminariorum, Collegiorum, piarum Communitatum, Hospitalium, Carcerum et similium. Si vero, juxta Kalendarium Ecclesiae aut Oratoriorum praefatorum, Missae votivae vel aliae permittantur, easdem celebrare valeant, servatis Rubricis et Decretis.

6. All priests, secular and regular, are to celebrate their Masses, even those proper to Regulars, entirely according to the Calendar of the church or public oratory in which they celebrate; excluding, however, the particular rites proper to Orders and churches. The same is to be observed in a semi-public oratory, or the principal chapel of seminaries, colleges, pious communities, hospitals, prisons and the like. If, according to the Calendar of the church or of these oratories, votive or other Masses are allowed, they may celebrate them, observing the Rubrics and Decrees.

VIII De MemoriisOf Commemorations

In faciendis memoriis servetur ordo, ut in Officio diei et in Rubricis Breviarii statuitur.

In making Commemorations the order laid down in the Office of the day and in the Rubrics of the Breviary is kept.

1. In omnibus Dominicis, in quibus facta fuerit Memoria Festi Totius Duplicis aut Duplicis, vel Octavae privilegiatae aut diei octavae Solemnis, in Missa Dominicae omittenda est tertia Oratio.

1. On all Sundays on which a Commemoration has been made of a feast Totum Duplex or Duplex, of a privileged Octave or of a solemn Octave day, the third Prayer is omitted in the Mass of the Sunday.

2. In Vigilia Pentecostes et in Dominica Palmarum nihil fit in Missa de quolibet Officio ad Laudes commemorato. Item in Festis I classis ad Missam conventualem vel cantatam nihil fit de Totis Duplicibus communibus et infra, vel de Octavis non privilegiatis in Laudibus commemoratis, neque in Festis II classis de Festis trium Lectionum vel de Octavis trium Lectionum aut solius Memoriae. Sed de his omnibus Memoria fit tantum in Missis privatis. De festis autem Domini in quavis Dominica minore vel in Vigilia Epiphaniae occurrentibus fit memoria etiam in Missis cantatis vel conventualibus Totius Duplicis I classis, si facta fuerit in Officio.

2. On the Vigil of Pentecost and on Palm Sunday nothing is done in the Mass of any Office commemorated at Lauds. Likewise on feasts of the I class nothing is done at the conventual or sung Mass of common feasts Totum Duplex and below, or of non-privileged Octaves commemorated at Lauds; nor on feasts of the II class of feasts of three Lessons or of Octaves of three Lessons or of a Commemoration only. All of these are commemorated only in private Masses. But feasts of our Lord falling on any lesser Sunday or on the Vigil of the Epiphany are commemorated even in the sung or conventual Masses of a feast Totum Duplex of the I class, if they were commemorated in the Office.

3. Ubi una tantum Missa habeatur et obligatio Missae conventualis non urgeat, in Missis votivis solemnibus pro re gravi et publica simul causa et in Missis de Toto Duplici I classis impedito, quod cum magno populi concursu juxta tit. VII, num. 2, celebratur, et in Missis de solemnitate externa Totius Duplicis item I classis in Dominicam, juxta eumdem tit. VII, num. 3, translata, fit tantum Memoria de Toto Duplici II classis, de Dominica quavis, etiam anticipata, et, ante memoriam Dominicae minoris vel Vigiliae Epiphaniae, de quolibet festo Domini occurrente, de Feria majori, de Rogationibus et de aliqua ex Vigiliis aut Octavis privilegiatis. In Missa autem de Toto Duplici II classis impedito, quod cum magno populi concursu, juxta tit. VII, num. 2, celebretur, et in Missis de solemnitate externa Totius Duplicis item II classis in Dominicam translata, fiunt omnes Memoriae ea die occurrentes praeterquam de die infra Octavam non privilegiatam et de Festo vel die Octava trium Lectionum. Ubi vero plures habeantur Missae cantatae aut conventuales, in Missa diei vel, si non habeatur, in prima earum dicuntur omnes Memoriae occurrentes, et in Missis votivis solemnibus etc. supra recensitis una tantum dicitur Oratio.

3. Where there is only one Mass and the obligation of a conventual Mass does not press: in solemn votive Masses for a grave and public cause, in Masses of an impeded feast Totum Duplex of the I class celebrated with a great concourse of people according to title VII, n. 2, and in Masses of the external solemnity of a feast Totum Duplex of the I class transferred to a Sunday according to title VII, n. 3, the only Commemorations made are of a feast Totum Duplex of the II class, of any Sunday (even anticipated), and, before the Commemoration of a lesser Sunday or of the Vigil of the Epiphany, of any feast of our Lord that occurs, of a greater Feria, of the Rogations, and of a privileged Vigil or Octave. In the Mass of an impeded feast Totum Duplex of the II class celebrated with a great concourse of people according to title VII, n. 2, and in Masses of the external solemnity of a feast Totum Duplex of the II class transferred to a Sunday, all the Commemorations occurring that day are made, except those of a day within a non-privileged Octave and of a feast or Octave day of three Lessons. Where there are several sung or conventual Masses, all the occurring Commemorations are said in the Mass of the day, or, if there is none, in the first of them; and in the solemn votive Masses and the others listed above only one Prayer is said.

IX De Officio, seu Introitu Missae, Kyrie eleison, et Gloria in excelsis DeoOf the Office or Introit of the Mass, the Kyrie eleison and the Gloria in excelsis Deo

1. Introitus Missae, et Versus cum Gloria Patri, semper eodem modo dicuntur, semperque post Gloria, repetitur Introitus usque ad Versum, praeterquam in Missa pro Defunctis, et a Dominica Passionis usque ad Cenam Domini inclusive, quando de Tempore Missa celebratur, in quibus Gloria Patri omittitur, et post Versum immediate Introitus repetitur. Kyrie eleison post Introitum dicitur alternatim cum ministro, scilicet ter Kyrie eleison, ter Christe eleison, et ter Kyrie eleison.

1. The Introit of the Mass and the Verse with the Gloria Patri are always said in the same way, and after the Gloria the Introit is always repeated up to the Verse; except in the Mass for the Dead, and from Passion Sunday to Maundy Thursday inclusive when the Mass is of the Season, when the Gloria Patri is omitted and the Introit is repeated immediately after the Verse. The Kyrie eleison is said after the Introit alternately with the server: three times Kyrie eleison, three times Christe eleison and three times Kyrie eleison.

2. Gloria in excelsis Deo dicitur solum quotiescumque in Officio ante Laudes dictum fuerit Te Deum laudamus, praeterquam in Cena Domini, et Sabbato sancto, in quibus dicitur etiam Gloria in excelsis, licet in Officio non fuerit dictum Te Deum laudamus. Item in Missis votivis conventualibus, juxta tit. V, num. 1, ex privilegio in Ordine nostro per hebdomadam dici solitis, extra tamen Septuagesimam. In aliis vero Missis de Vigilia, vel quacumque Feria habente propriam Missam, quamvis celebrentur diebus, quibus in Officio dictum est Te Deum laudamus, non dicitur Gloria in excelsis, sed conformiter ad proprium ritum dicuntur; secus vero si Missa Dominicae resumatur illis diebus, quia fuit impedita, vel Missae votivae privatae dicantur: quia illae conformantur ritui diei, de quo factum est Officium. Immo in Missis votivis solemnibus pro re gravi et publica simul causa dicitur Gloria in excelsis Deo etiam in diebus Feriarum.

2. The Gloria in excelsis Deo is said only when the Te Deum laudamus has been said in the Office before Lauds; except on Maundy Thursday and Holy Saturday, when the Gloria in excelsis is said although the Te Deum was not said in the Office. Likewise in the conventual votive Masses customarily said through the week in our Order by privilege, according to title V, n. 1, but not in Septuagesima. In other Masses of a Vigil, or of any Feria that has its own Mass, even when they are celebrated on days on which the Te Deum was said in the Office, the Gloria in excelsis is not said, for they are said according to their own rite; it is otherwise if the Mass of a Sunday is resumed on those days because it was impeded, or if private votive Masses are said, for these follow the rite of the day whose Office was said. Indeed in solemn votive Masses for a grave and public cause the Gloria in excelsis Deo is said even on Ferias.

X De numero, et ordine Orationum ordinarie dicendarum, praesertim in Missa conventualiOf the number and order of the Prayers ordinarily said, especially at the conventual Mass

In Missa conventuali tres Orationes tantum dicuntur, nisi plures memoriarum necessitas exigat, quo casu tot Orationes dicuntur, quot memoriae in Laudibus dictae fuerint, et eodem ordine.

At the conventual Mass only three Prayers are said, unless the number of Commemorations requires more; in that case as many Prayers are said as Commemorations were made at Lauds, and in the same order.

1. In Missis conventualibus et privatis ritus Semiduplicis et infra (etiam ferialibus) et in Missis votivis, quae pro re gravi et publica simul causa non sint, nisi facta fuerit Memoria Totius Duplicis, Duplicis vel Octavae solemnis, Orationes ad supplendum numerum ternarium sumantur ex I Dominica respectivi temporis modo sequenti: a) Tempore Adventus, quando Memoria facta fuerit, 3a Oratio erit de B. M. V. Deus qui de beatae Mariae, nisi dicenda occurrat Memoria B. Mariae V., quia tunc 3a Oratio erit Deus qui caritatis; b) A die 2 Januarii usque ad diem octavam Epiphaniae exclusive quotidie (praeterquam in festo S. Familiae) post alias Memorias dicitur Oratio B. M. V. Deus qui salutis. Die 4 Januarii 2a Oratio erit de B. M. V., 3a pro Papa. In Vigilia Epiphaniae dicuntur Orationes ut in Missali. c) Tempore Paschali usque ad Vigiliam Ascensionis inclusive, quando Memoria facta fuerit, 3a Oratio erit de Cruce Deus qui pro nobis Filium, quae, utpote in Laudibus dicta, etiam addenda est, si plures Memoriae dicendae occurrant; d) Ab Octava Epiphaniae usque ad Feriam V in Cena Domini et ab Octava Cordis Jesu usque ad Adventum, quando Memoria facta fuerit, 3a Oratio erit A cunctis, nisi dicenda occurrat Memoria de B. Maria V. vel de Omnibus Sanctis, quia in hoc casu 3a Oratio erit ad libitum; tempore vero Septuagesimae usque ad Cenam Domini Deus, qui caritatis; e) Praedictis diebus, quando duae vel plures Memoriae factae fuerint, nulla Oratio ex Dominica addenda est, exceptis casibus sub b) et c) notatis. Si vero nulla Memoria dicenda occurrat, 2a et 3a Oratio sumuntur ex I Dominica respectivi temporis; tunc vero in Missis votivis B. Mariae V. (in Sabbato) loco 2ae vel 3ae Orationis de B. Maria V. vel A cunctis, sumitur Oratio Ecclesiae, num. 7. f) In Vigiliis privilegiatis Nativitatis D. N. J. C. et Epiphaniae Orationes dicuntur ut in Missali. Dominica in Ramis Palmarum et in Vigilia Pentecostes una tantum dicitur Oratio, etiamsi quae ad Laudes facta fuerit Memoria. g) In Missis votivis, quae pro re gravi et publica simul causa non sint, secundo loco semper addatur Memoria Officii diei, nisi huic praeponenda sit Memoria a Missa inseparabilis; h) Quando duae Missae conventuales occurrunt, etiam adhibentur praedictae Orationes ad supplendum numerum ternarium, quamvis tunc eaedem in utraque Missa forsan dicendae sint.

1. In conventual and private Masses of the rite Semiduplex and below (ferial ones too), and in votive Masses not for a grave and public cause, unless a Commemoration has been made of a feast Totum Duplex, Duplex or of a solemn Octave, the Prayers that complete the number three are taken from the first Sunday of the season concerned, as follows: a) In Advent, when a Commemoration has been made, the third Prayer is of Our Lady, Deus qui de beatae Mariae; unless a Commemoration of Our Lady is to be said, for then the third Prayer is Deus qui caritatis. b) From 2 January to the Octave day of the Epiphany exclusive, every day (except on the feast of the Holy Family), after the other Commemorations, the Prayer of Our Lady Deus qui salutis is said. On 4 January the second Prayer is of Our Lady, the third for the Pope. On the Vigil of the Epiphany the Prayers are said as in the Missal. c) In Paschaltide up to the Vigil of the Ascension inclusive, when a Commemoration has been made, the third Prayer is of the Cross, Deus qui pro nobis Filium, which, having been said at Lauds, is also added when several Commemorations are to be said. d) From the Octave of the Epiphany to Maundy Thursday, and from the Octave of the Sacred Heart to Advent, when a Commemoration has been made, the third Prayer is A cunctis; unless a Commemoration of Our Lady or of All Saints is to be said, for in that case the third Prayer is at choice; and in Septuagesima, up to Maundy Thursday, Deus qui caritatis. e) On these days, when two or more Commemorations have been made, no Prayer is added from the Sunday, except in the cases noted under b) and c). If no Commemoration is to be said, the second and third Prayers are taken from the first Sunday of the season concerned; but then in the votive Masses of Our Lady (on Saturday), instead of the second or third Prayer, of Our Lady or A cunctis, the Prayer for the Church, n. 7, is taken. f) On the privileged Vigils of the Nativity of our Lord and of the Epiphany the Prayers are said as in the Missal. On Palm Sunday and on the Vigil of Pentecost only one Prayer is said, even if some Commemoration was made at Lauds. g) In votive Masses not for a grave and public cause the Commemoration of the Office of the day is always added in the second place, unless a Commemoration inseparable from the Mass must come before it. h) When two conventual Masses occur, these Prayers are also used to complete the number three, although they may then have to be said in both Masses.

2. In anniversario propriae ordinationis sacerdotalis a die fixa mensis computando, si Vigilia Nativitatis vel Pentecostes, Dominica Palmarum aut Totum Duplex I classis non occurrerit, secus autem in proximiori sequenti die, quae a Toto Duplici item I classis sit libera, cuivis Sacerdoti licet, extra Missas Defunctorum, et post Orationes a Rubricis praescriptas, addere Orationem pro seipso Sacerdote, ut inter Orationes diversas.

2. On the anniversary of his own ordination to the priesthood, counted from the fixed day of the month, if the Vigil of Christmas or of Pentecost, Palm Sunday or a feast Totum Duplex of the I class does not occur (otherwise on the nearest following day free of a feast Totum Duplex of the I class), any priest may add, except in Masses for the Dead and after the Prayers prescribed by the Rubrics, the Prayer for the priest himself, as among the various Prayers.

3. Orationes seu Collectae ab Ordinario imperatae numquam plures sint quam duae; eaeque ponuntur, et numquam sub unica conclusione cum Oratione Missae, ultimo loco post Orationes a Rubricis praescriptas, vel loco Orationis ad libitum pro diversitate Temporum assignatae, quoties nempe tertia Oratio ad libitum sit eligenda. Omitti tamen debent, propter respectivam Missam vel Memoriam, in omnibus Totis Duplicibus I et II classis, in Dominicis majoribus, in Feriis, Vigiliis et Octavis privilegiatis, in Missis votivis pro re gravi et publica simul causa vel ad instar solemnium, ut supra, celebratis, et quoties in Missa, juxta Rubricas, jam quatuor dictae sint Orationes; quinimmo Collectae pro Defunctis omitti insuper debent Tempore Paschali, et in omnibus Missis quae de Officio Simplici et supra, aut eorum Memoria celebrantur. Et, si jussa fuerit Oratio contra persecutores Ecclesiae vel pro Papa, in diebus in quibus alterutra a Rubricis praescribitur, unica Oratione utrumque adimpletur praeceptum. Si tamen, jubente Ordinario, hujusmodi Collectae fuerint dicendae pro re gravi, omittuntur tantum in Vigiliis Nativitatis et Pentecostes, in Dominica Palmarum, et in omnibus Totis Duplicibus I classis. Quod si expresse eae ordinentur etiam in Totis Duplicibus I classis, tunc omittuntur in Nativitate Domini, Epiphania Domini, Feria V in Cena Domini, Sabbato Sancto, Dominica Resurrectionis, Ascensione Domini, Pentecoste, Festo SS. Trinitatis, Festo SS. Corporis Christi, SS. Cordis Jesu et D. N. J. Christi Regis.

3. Prayers or Collects ordered by the Ordinary are never to be more than two; they are put, never under one conclusion with the Prayer of the Mass, in the last place after the Prayers prescribed by the Rubrics, or in place of the Prayer at choice assigned for the various seasons, namely whenever the third Prayer is at choice. They must be omitted, however, because of the Mass or Commemoration concerned, on all feasts Totum Duplex of the I and II class, on greater Sundays, on privileged Ferias, Vigils and Octaves, in votive Masses celebrated for a grave and public cause or after the manner of solemn ones, as above, and whenever four Prayers have already been said in the Mass according to the Rubrics; moreover Collects for the Dead must also be omitted in Paschaltide and in all Masses celebrated of an Office Simplex and above or with its Commemoration. And if the Prayer against the persecutors of the Church or for the Pope has been ordered, on days on which either is prescribed by the Rubrics, both commands are fulfilled with one Prayer. If, however, by the Ordinary's command such Collects are to be said for a grave cause, they are omitted only on the Vigils of Christmas and Pentecost, on Palm Sunday and on all feasts Totum Duplex of the I class. If they are expressly ordered even on feasts Totum Duplex of the I class, they are then omitted on Christmas, the Epiphany, Maundy Thursday, Holy Saturday, Easter Sunday, the Ascension, Pentecost, the feasts of the Holy Trinity, Corpus Christi, the Sacred Heart and Christ the King.

4. Ordo autem in dicendis hujusmodi Collectis, praeter Collectam pro Defunctis, penultimo semper loco inter Orationes etiam ad Celebrantis libitum permissas collocandam, est sequens: Prius ponitur Collecta dicenda pro re gravi, si habeatur. Si vero ambae Collectae sint jussae pro re gravi, prius ponitur, juxta ordinem Litaniarum, Collecta de aliquo Mysterio vel Sancto, quae praecepta fuerit; postea quae desumenda sit ex Missis votivis diversis vel ex Orationibus quae in fine Missalis habentur, servato, si ambae sint hujusmodi, eodem ordine quo in Missali prostant.

4. The order in saying these Collects (apart from the Collect for the Dead, which is always put in the last place but one among the Prayers, those allowed at the celebrant's choice included) is this: first the Collect to be said for a grave cause, if there is one. If both Collects are ordered for a grave cause, first, in the order of the Litanies, the Collect of a Mystery or Saint that was prescribed; then the one to be taken from the various votive Masses or from the Prayers at the end of the Missal, keeping, if both are of this kind, the order in which they stand in the Missal.

5. In omnibus Missis lectis, Missa quidem conventuali excepta, quae in die ritus trium Lectionum, extra Ferias majores privilegiatas, vel de occurrenti Officio celebrentur, vel ex votivis pro re non gravi nec publica simul causa eligantur, plures dici possunt ad Celebrantis libitum Orationes; cauto tamen semper ut Orationes hujusmodi: 1) post Orationes a Rubricis praescriptas et Collectas ab Ordinario imperatas locum habeant; et cum eis septenarium numerum non excedant, atque imparem praeterea numerum retineant vel inducant; 2) inter se illum ordinem servent qui supra, num. 4, pro duabus Collectis, cum ambae sint pro re gravi aut non gravi, est praefinitus.

5. In all read Masses (except the conventual Mass) celebrated on a day of the rite of three Lessons, outside the greater privileged Ferias, whether of the occurring Office or chosen from the votive Masses for a cause that is not grave and public, more Prayers may be said at the celebrant's choice; always taking care that such Prayers 1) come after the Prayers prescribed by the Rubrics and the Collects ordered by the Ordinary, and together with them do not exceed seven, and keep or make an odd number; 2) keep among themselves the order laid down in n. 4 above for two Collects, when both are for a grave cause or both not.

XI Modus terminandi OrationesHow the Prayers are concluded

1. Quotiescumque autem dicuntur plures Orationes, prima tantum, et ultima cum sua peroratione, seu conclusione terminantur; et ante primam, et secundam Orationem tantum dicitur Oremus: et ante primam dicitur etiam Dominus vobiscum, nisi aliter notetur, et praeterquam in Secretis, loco cujus ante primam Secretam dicitur Domine exaudi orationem meam.

1. Whenever several Prayers are said, only the first and the last are finished with their peroration or conclusion; Oremus is said only before the first and the second Prayer; and before the first Dominus vobiscum is also said, unless otherwise noted, and except at the Secrets, where instead of it Domine exaudi orationem meam is said before the first Secret.

2. In peroratione, seu conclusione Orationum servetur hic modus terminandi: Si Oratio dirigatur ad Patrem, concluditur Per Dominum nostrum; et si ad Filium, Qui vivis et regnas cum Deo Patre; si in principio Orationis fiat mentio Filii, terminatur Per eumdem Dominum nostrum; si in fine Orationis fiat ejus mentio, terminatur per Qui tecum vivit; si in Oratione facta fuerit mentio Spiritus Sancti, terminatur in unitate ejusdem Spiritus Sancti: denique si fiat mentio B. Mariae, dicendo Dei Genitricem, in fine terminatur Per eumdem Dominum, vel Qui tecum vivit.

2. In the peroration or conclusion of the Prayers this way of ending is kept: if the Prayer is addressed to the Father, it ends Per Dominum nostrum; if to the Son, Qui vivis et regnas cum Deo Patre; if the Son is mentioned at the beginning of the Prayer, it ends Per eumdem Dominum nostrum; if He is mentioned at the end, Qui tecum vivit; if the Holy Ghost has been mentioned in the Prayer, it ends in unitate ejusdem Spiritus Sancti; lastly, if Blessed Mary is mentioned as Mother of God, it ends Per eumdem Dominum, or Qui tecum vivit.

XII De Oratione in Missis DefunctorumOf the Prayer in Masses for the Dead

1. In omnibus Missis quae tit. VI, num. 3-7 recensentur, et quandocumque pro Defunctis solemniter, nempe sub ritu qui Duplici respondet, celebratur, una tantum dicitur Oratio; in reliquis tres dicuntur ordine infra posito num. 4 et sequentibus.

1. In all the Masses listed in title VI, nn. 3-7, and whenever Mass for the Dead is celebrated solemnly, that is, with the rite corresponding to a Duplex, only one Prayer is said; in the others three are said, in the order given below in n. 4 and following.

2. In die obitus, seu depositionis Defuncti et opportuniori die post acceptum mortis nuntium, si Papa fuerit, Oratio erit Deus, qui inter summos, quae Oratio dicitur etiam in ejus Anniversario. Si Episcopus, vel Sacerdos, Deus, qui inter apostolicos, nisi quod pro Episcopo dicitur pontificali, pro Sacerdote sacerdotali. Si vero alius fuerit vel alia, Oratio erit Deus, cui proprium.

2. On the day of death or burial of the deceased, and on a convenient day after news of the death has been received: for a Pope the Prayer is Deus, qui inter summos, which is also said on his Anniversary; for a Bishop or a priest, Deus, qui inter apostolicos, saying pontificali for a Bishop and sacerdotali for a priest; for any other man or woman, Deus, cui proprium.

3. In die tertio, septimo, et trigesimo depositionis Defuncti, si fuerit Papa, vel Episcopus, vel Sacerdos, Oratio erit ut pro die obitus num. 2 notatum est. Si vero alius fuerit vel alia, Oratio erit Quaesumus Domine, ut animae.

3. On the third, seventh and thirtieth day from the burial, for a Pope, a Bishop or a priest the Prayer is as noted for the day of death in n. 2; for any other man or woman it is Quaesumus Domine, ut animae.

4. Si pro aliquo uno Defuncto specialiter celebratur extra diem obitus vel depositionis, si fuerit Papa vel Episcopus, vel Sacerdos, prima Oratio erit ut in die obitus ipsorum, secunda Deus, veniae largitor, tertia Omnipotens sempiterne Deus, qui vivorum. Si vero alius fuerit, prima, si vir, Inclina, Domine; si femina, Quaesumus, Domine, pro tua; secunda et tertia non mutantur.

4. If Mass is celebrated specially for one deceased person on a day other than that of death or burial: for a Pope, a Bishop or a priest the first Prayer is as on the day of their death, the second Deus, veniae largitor, the third Omnipotens sempiterne Deus, qui vivorum. For anyone else the first is, for a man, Inclina, Domine; for a woman, Quaesumus, Domine, pro tua; the second and third are unchanged.

5. Si pro omnibus Defunctis generaliter celebratur, prima Oratio erit Fidelium Deus, secunda et tertia ut supra num. 4. Si vero pro pluribus tantum, prima Oratio erit Animabus, quaesumus, vel Propitiare, quaesumus; secunda et tertia non mutantur. Si autem ignoretur pro quo vel pro quibus celebrandum sit, prima Oratio erit Deus, qui inter Apostolicos; secunda et tertia non mutantur.

5. If Mass is celebrated for all the dead in general, the first Prayer is Fidelium Deus, the second and third as in n. 4. If for several only, the first Prayer is Animabus, quaesumus, or Propitiare, quaesumus; the second and third are unchanged. If it is not known for whom Mass is to be celebrated, the first Prayer is Deus, qui inter Apostolicos; the second and third are unchanged.

6. Si prima die cujusque hebdomadae juxta Rubricas, tit. VI, num. 2, non impedita, pro fratribus, familiaribus, et benefactoribus celebretur, prima Oratio erit Deus, veniae largitor, secunda Deus, qui nos Patrem, tertia non mutabitur.

6. If Mass is celebrated for the brethren, familiars and benefactors on the first unimpeded day of the week, according to title VI, n. 2, the first Prayer is Deus, veniae largitor, the second Deus, qui nos Patrem; the third is unchanged.

7. In quatuor Anniversariis in Ordine nostro singulis annis dici solitis, prima Oratio erit Deus Indulgentiarum, in plurali numero; secunda et tertia ut supra num. 4, praeterquam in duobus, in quibus secunda Oratio in mense Februario erit Deus, qui nos Patrem, et in mense Julio secunda erit Deus, cujus miseratione, tertia vero non mutatur.

7. In the four Anniversaries customarily kept every year in our Order, the first Prayer is Deus Indulgentiarum, in the plural; the second and third as in n. 4, except in two of them: in the February one the second Prayer is Deus, qui nos Patrem, and in the July one the second is Deus, cujus miseratione; the third is unchanged.

8. Si autem simul evenerit dies obitus, seu depositionis Defuncti, et Anniversarium, prima Oratio dicitur pro praesenti Defuncto, secunda pro Anniversario.

8. If the day of death or burial of someone deceased and an Anniversary fall together, the first Prayer is said for the deceased now present, the second for the Anniversary.

9. Si in die Animarum celebranda sit Missa exsequialis corpore praesente, haec erit prima de Commemoratione omnium fidelium defunctorum, addita sub una conclusione Oratione pro defuncto ut in die obitus.

9. If a funeral Mass with the body present is to be celebrated on All Souls' Day, it is the first Mass of the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed, with the Prayer for the deceased, as on the day of death, added under one conclusion.

XIII De Epistola, Graduali seu Responsorio, Alleluia, Tractu et Sequentia, ac de EvangelioOf the Epistle, the Gradual or Responsory, the Alleluia, the Tract and the Sequence, and of the Gospel

1. Post ultimam Orationem dicitur Epistola, qua finita, nihil respondetur a ministris. Post Epistolam dicitur semper Graduale, quod apud nos vocatur Responsorium, praeterquam a Sabbato Dominicae in Albis inclusive usque ad Octavam Pentecostes, cujus loco, toto isto tempore, tam in Missis de Tempore, quam de festis quibuscumque, dicuntur duo Versus cum quatuor Alleluia, hoc ordine: ante primum Versum, duo dicuntur Alleluia, et post Versum aliud Alleluia: deinde secundus Versus, et post Versum aliud Alleluia. Quando vero dicitur Sequentia, non dicitur post ultimum Versum Alleluia, sed post Sequentiam. Excipiuntur tamen festa trium Lectionum, et Vigilia Ascensionis, et dies feriales, in quibus dicitur tantum semel Alleluia ante unicum Versum, et semel post Versum: qui Versus in Missis de Feriis ex duobus positis pro Dominica alternatim desumi debet.

1. After the last Prayer the Epistle is said, and when it is finished the ministers make no answer. After the Epistle the Gradual, which with us is called the Responsory, is always said, except from the Saturday before Low Sunday inclusive to the Octave of Pentecost; in its place, through all that time, in Masses of the Season and of any feast whatever, two Verses are said with four Alleluias, in this order: before the first Verse two Alleluias, and after the Verse another Alleluia; then the second Verse, and after it another Alleluia. When the Sequence is said, the Alleluia is not said after the last Verse but after the Sequence. Excepted are feasts of three Lessons, the Vigil of the Ascension and ferial days, on which Alleluia is said only once before the single Verse and once after it; on Ferias this Verse is to be taken alternately from the two given for the Sunday.

2. Excipiuntur etiam Missae Rogationum, in quibus dicitur unum tantum Alleluia ante Versum, et post non repetitur.

2. Also excepted are the Masses of the Rogations, in which only one Alleluia is said before the Verse, and it is not repeated after it.

3. Similiter post quamlibet Lectionem in quarta Feria, et Sabbato Quatuor Temporum Pentecostes dicuntur Alleluia eo modo, quo ibidem signantur.

3. Similarly, after each Lesson on the Wednesday and Saturday of the Ember days of Pentecost the Alleluias are said in the way marked there.

4. A festo vero sanctissimae Trinitatis inclusive usque ad Septuagesimam exclusive, dum dicitur Responsorium, dicuntur tria Alleluia bis ante ultimum Versum, et semel post Versum (si autem dicitur Sequentia, fiat ut supra), et hoc tam in Dominicis, quam in omnibus Festis, et Octavis, et Missis votivis, ex privilegio, vel consuetudine in Ordine nostro per hebdomadam dici solitis.

4. From the feast of the Holy Trinity inclusive to Septuagesima exclusive, when the Responsory is said, three Alleluias are said: twice before the last Verse and once after the Verse (if the Sequence is said, it is done as above); and this on Sundays as well as on all feasts and Octaves, and in the votive Masses customarily said through the week in our Order by privilege or custom.

5. In festis autem trium Lectionum duo tantum Alleluia dicuntur cum unico Versu post Responsorium, semel ante Versum, et semel post Versum.

5. On feasts of three Lessons only two Alleluias are said, with a single Verse, after the Responsory: once before the Verse and once after it.

6. In Feriis vero totaliter omittuntur, uti etiam in Missa Dominicae, quae dicitur praedictis ferialibus diebus, et in festo Innocentium, quando non cadit in Dominicam: item in Quatuor Temporibus et Vigiliis, quocumque die celebrentur, exceptis Vigilia Nativitatis Domini, si occurrerit in Dominica, et Vigilia Epiphaniae, quocumque die occurrerit, in quibus Alleluia dicuntur eo modo, quo appositum est in illis Missis.

6. On Ferias they are omitted altogether, as also in the Mass of the Sunday said on those ferial days, and on the feast of the Innocents when it does not fall on a Sunday; likewise on Ember days and Vigils, on whatever day they are kept, except the Vigil of Christmas when it falls on a Sunday and the Vigil of the Epiphany on whatever day it falls, on which the Alleluias are said as they are set in those Masses.

7. A Septuagesima vero usque ad Sabbatum Paschae exclusive, tam de Tempore, quam de Festis, nullum dicitur Alleluia: sed post Responsorium, et unicum Versum, omisso Alleluia, et Versu sequenti, dicitur Tractus, praeterquam in festis trium Lectionum, in quibus numquam dicitur, et in Missis Dominicae, quas infra Septuagesimam usque ad Feriam quartam Cinerum in diebus ferialibus dici contingat. Excipiuntur etiam quaedam Feriae infra diem Cinerum, et Quadragesimam, in quibus Tractus non apponitur. Insuper dicitur etiam multiplex Tractus Sabbato Sancto et in Vigilia Pentecostes post Lectiones, ut ibidem assignatur: item in Sabbatis Quatuor Temporum Adventus, et Septembris.

7. From Septuagesima to Holy Saturday exclusive, in Masses of the Season and of feasts alike, no Alleluia is said; but after the Responsory and its single Verse, the Alleluia and the following Verse being left out, the Tract is said; except on feasts of three Lessons, on which it is never said, and in the Sunday Masses said on ferial days from Septuagesima to Ash Wednesday. Also excepted are certain Ferias from Ash Wednesday and in Lent which have no Tract. Several Tracts are said on Holy Saturday and on the Vigil of Pentecost after the Lessons, as assigned there; likewise on the Ember Saturdays of Advent and September.

8. Quandocumque autem Tractus dicitur, numquam dicitur Sequentia (nisi in Compassione B. Virginis Mariae); dicitur autem haec illis diebus, in quibus apponitur dicenda.

8. Whenever the Tract is said, the Sequence is never said (except on the Compassion of the Blessed Virgin Mary); the Sequence is said on the days on which it is set down to be said.

9. Sequentia vero pro Mortuis dicitur in omnibus Missis cum unica Oratione, et in Missis quotidianis in cantu et conventualibus; in aliis Missis quotidianis lectis dici vel omitti potest ad libitum Celebrantis.

9. The Sequence for the Dead is said in all Masses with a single Prayer, and in the daily Masses that are sung and in conventual ones; in other daily read Masses it may be said or omitted at the celebrant's choice.

10. Dicto Responsorio, vel Alleluia, vel Tractu, vel Sequentia, dicitur Evangelium, in cujus principio praemittitur: Dominus vobiscum. ℟. Et cum spiritu tuo. Deinde: Sequentia sancti Evangelii secundum, etc. ℟. Gloria tibi Domine. Post Evangelium dicitur Credo, si tamen dicendum est, prout notatur titulo sequenti.

10. After the Responsory, the Alleluia, the Tract or the Sequence, the Gospel is said, beginning with Dominus vobiscum. ℟. Et cum spiritu tuo. Then: Sequentia sancti Evangelii secundum, etc. ℟. Gloria tibi Domine. After the Gospel the Credo is said, if it is to be said, as noted in the next title.

Credo, quod vocatur Symbolum Apostolorum, dicitur in Nativitate Domini ad omnes Missas, et quotidie per Octavam, etiam in festis Sanctorum: In Circumcisione Domini: In die Epiphaniae, et per Octavam: Feria quinta in Cena Domini: In die Paschae, et per Octavam: In Ascensione, et per Octavam: In die Pentecostes, et per Octavam: In die SS. Corporis Christi, et per Octavam: In festo SS. Cordis Jesu et per Octavam: In omnibus Dominicis per annum, etiamsi in illis fiat de Festo, in quo alias non diceretur: In omnibus festis B. Mariae: In festis duodecim Apostolorum, et Evangelistarum, et in die octava SS. Apostolorum Petri et Pauli: In utraque Cathedra S. Petri, et in festo S. Petri ad Vincula: In festis Conversionis, et Commemorationis S. Pauli Apostoli: In festo S. Joannis ante portam Latinam: In festo S. Barnabae Apostoli: In festis Inventionis, et Exaltationis S. Crucis: In festis SS. Nominis Jesu et S. Familiae: In festo Coronae Domini: In festo Pretiosissimi Sanguinis: In Transfiguratione Domini: In festis Angelorum: In utroque festo S. Joseph, Sponsi B. Mariae Virginis: In festo S. Mariae Magdalenae: In triplici festo B. Dominici P. N.: In singulis festis Sanctorum canonizatorum Ordinis nostri: In triplici festo S. Catharinae Senensis: In festo principali S. Francisci Assisinatis: In festis Doctorum Ecclesiae, ut Augustini, Hieronymi, Gregorii Papae, Leonis Magni, in festo S. Ambrosii, in utroque festo S. Thomae de Aquino, et in festo S. Bonaventurae: additis festis S. Hilarii, S. Francisci Salesii, S. Petri Damiani, S. Isidori, S. Anselmi, S. Bedae Venerabilis, S. Alphonsi Mariae de Ligorio, et S. Bernardi: Item in festis Doctorum Joannis Chrysostomi, Athanasii, Cyrilli Alexandrini, Cyrilli Hierosolymitani, Joannis Damasceni et Ephraem Syri, Gregorii Nazianzeni, et Basilii: In festo Omnium Sanctorum: In Dedicatione SS. Salvatoris, et SS. Apostolorum Petri et Pauli: In festo Dedicationis propriae Ecclesiae, et in Anniversario ejusdem: In die Consecrationis Ecclesiae vel Altaris: In die Creationis, et Coronationis summi Pontificis: In singulis festis Patronorum loci, vel Tituli Ecclesiarum nostrarum; et in ipsa Ecclesia, ubi habetur Corpus, vel insignis Reliquia Sancti, de quo Missa celebratur. Item si aliqua Missa in die, qua cantari debet Credo, occurrerit celebranda, nisi fuerit pro Defunctis, aut de Vigilia Sancti. Aliis vero diebus non dicatur, nisi in Missis solemnibus Votivis vel quacumque Feria habente propriam Missam, quae pro re gravi, et publica simul causa, aut necessitate celebrantur.

The Credo, which is called the Symbol of the Apostles, is said: on Christmas Day at all the Masses, and daily through the Octave, even on the feasts of Saints; on the Circumcision; on the Epiphany and through its Octave; on Maundy Thursday; on Easter Day and through its Octave; on the Ascension and through its Octave; on Pentecost and through its Octave; on Corpus Christi and through its Octave; on the feast of the Sacred Heart and through its Octave; on all Sundays of the year, even when the Office is of a feast on which it would not otherwise be said; on all feasts of Our Lady; on the feasts of the twelve Apostles and of the Evangelists, and on the Octave day of the Apostles Peter and Paul; on both feasts of the Chair of St Peter and on St Peter's Chains; on the feasts of the Conversion and of the Commemoration of St Paul the Apostle; on the feast of St John before the Latin Gate; on the feast of St Barnabas the Apostle; on the feasts of the Finding and of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross; on the feasts of the Holy Name of Jesus and of the Holy Family; on the feast of the Crown of our Lord; on the feast of the Most Precious Blood; on the Transfiguration; on the feasts of the Angels; on both feasts of St Joseph, Spouse of the Blessed Virgin Mary; on the feast of St Mary Magdalen; on the three feasts of our blessed Father Dominic; on each feast of the canonized Saints of our Order; on the three feasts of St Catherine of Siena; on the principal feast of St Francis of Assisi; on the feasts of the Doctors of the Church, such as Augustine, Jerome, Gregory the Pope, Leo the Great, on the feast of St Ambrose, on both feasts of St Thomas Aquinas and on the feast of St Bonaventure, with the feasts added of St Hilary, St Francis de Sales, St Peter Damian, St Isidore, St Anselm, St Bede the Venerable, St Alphonsus Mary de Liguori and St Bernard; likewise on the feasts of the Doctors John Chrysostom, Athanasius, Cyril of Alexandria, Cyril of Jerusalem, John Damascene and Ephrem the Syrian, Gregory Nazianzen and Basil; on the feast of All Saints; on the Dedication of the Holy Saviour and of the Apostles Peter and Paul; on the feast of the Dedication of the church itself and on its Anniversary; on the day of the Consecration of a church or altar; on the day of the Election and of the Coronation of the Supreme Pontiff; on each feast of the Patrons of the place or of the Title of our churches; and in the church itself where the body or a notable relic is kept of the Saint whose Mass is celebrated. Likewise if any Mass is to be celebrated on a day on which the Credo must be sung, unless it is for the Dead or of the Vigil of a Saint. On other days it is not said, except in solemn votive Masses, or of any Feria with a Mass of its own, celebrated for a grave and public cause or necessity.

XV De Offertorio, Secretis, Praefationibus, et CanoneOf the Offertory, the Secrets, the Prefaces and the Canon

1. Post Credo, vel si non sit dicendum, post Evangelium, dicitur Dominus vobiscum. Oremus. Deinde dicitur Offertorium: postea fit oblatio cum Oratione, ut in modo dicendi Missam notatur. Oblatione facta, et Oratione post ablutionem digitorum finita cum Orate fratres, dicuntur Orationes secretae, secundum numerum Orationum, quae clara voce in principio Missae dictae sunt; et ante primam Orationem non dicitur Dominus vobiscum, sed Domine exaudi orationem meam: et clamor meus ad te veniat, et Oremus. Ante secundam vero dicitur tantum Oremus. Terminatur autem prima, et ultima Oratio eo modo, quo supra dictum est in Rubrica de Orationibus, et secreto dicendae sunt usque ad illa verba: Per omnia saecula saeculorum, quae clara voce dicuntur, et immediate simili voce incipitur, et prosequitur Praefatio.

1. After the Credo, or, if it is not said, after the Gospel, Dominus vobiscum. Oremus is said. Then the Offertory is said; after it the oblation is made with its Prayer, as noted in the manner of saying Mass. When the oblation is made, and the Prayer after the washing of the fingers is finished with Orate fratres, the secret Prayers are said, as many as the Prayers said aloud at the beginning of Mass; before the first Prayer Dominus vobiscum is not said, but Domine exaudi orationem meam: et clamor meus ad te veniat, and Oremus; before the second only Oremus. The first and the last Prayer are concluded as said above in the Rubric on the Prayers, and they are said secretly up to the words Per omnia saecula saeculorum, which are said aloud; and in the same voice the Preface is begun at once and continued.

2. Praefationes dicuntur, sicut de ordine servando in Missis praescriptum est in propriis locis, et quae in Quadragesima, tempore Passionis, et Paschali, et infra Octavas propriae assignantur, dicuntur etiam in Festis, quae illis temporibus celebrantur, nisi illa Festa propriam Praefationem habuerint. Praefationes vero Octavarum solemnium numquam in Missa Dominicae dicuntur, nisi quae infra hebdomadam resumitur. Si infra Octavam alicujus Festi propriam Praefationem habentis occurrerit aliud Festum majus, carens propria Praefatione, dicitur etiam Praefatio de Octava. Similiter in Missis votivis dicitur Praefatio propria, si propriam habeant: si autem non habuerint, dicitur Praefatio de Tempore, vel Octava, infra quam contigerit hujusmodi Missas celebrari: alias dicitur Praefatio communis. In omnibus Dominicis, extra Quadragesimam, tempus Paschale, et Octavas solemnissimas, dicitur Praefatio de Trinitate. In Missa Dominicae infra hebdomadam resumenda dicitur Praefatio Octavae vel communis. Et quando aliqua Missa votiva pro causa publica celebratur, dicatur in cantu solemni, ut in Toto Duplici. In Missis Defunctorum quocumque tempore semper dicatur Praefatio propria.

2. The Prefaces are said as prescribed in their proper places for the order to be kept in the Masses; and those assigned as proper to Lent, Passiontide, Paschaltide and the Octaves are also said on feasts celebrated in those times, unless those feasts have a Preface of their own. The Prefaces of solemn Octaves, however, are never said in the Mass of a Sunday, except when it is resumed during the week. If within the Octave of a feast that has its own Preface another, greater feast occurs which has none, the Preface of the Octave is said. Likewise in votive Masses the proper Preface is said if they have one; if not, the Preface of the Season or of the Octave within which such Masses happen to be celebrated; otherwise the common Preface. On all Sundays outside Lent, Paschaltide and the most solemn Octaves, the Preface of the Trinity is said. In the Mass of a Sunday resumed during the week, the Preface of the Octave or the common Preface is said. When a votive Mass is celebrated for a public cause, the Preface is sung in the solemn chant, as on a Totum Duplex. In Masses for the Dead, at any time, their own Preface is always said.

3. Post Praefationem incipit Canon Missae secreto dicendus, in quo serventur omnia ut in Ordine Missae. Ubi vero infra Memento, Communicantes et Hanc igitur, vel alia variari contingit, suo loco in propriis Missis adnotatur.

3. After the Preface the Canon of the Mass begins, to be said secretly, in which everything is done as in the Order of Mass. Where, after the Memento, the Communicantes and Hanc igitur or other parts vary, this is noted in its place in the proper Masses.

XVI De Communione, Orationibus post Communionem, Ite missa est, vel Benedicamus Domino, de Benedictione et Evangelio S. JoannisOf the Communion, the Prayers after Communion, Ite missa est or Benedicamus Domino, the Blessing and the Gospel of St John

1. Absoluto Canone, et aliis omnibus usque ad communionem, ea peracta, dicuntur Communio, et Orationes post communionem eo modo, et ordine, ut in principio Missae: et finita ultima, repetitur Dominus vobiscum. ℟. Et cum spiritu tuo: et postea dicitur Ite Missa est, vel Benedicamus Domino, pro temporis exigentia. Ite Missa est dicitur quotiescumque dictum est Gloria in excelsis. Et dum non dicitur, ejus loco dicitur Benedicamus Domino. ℟. Deo gratias. In Missis Defunctorum dicitur Requiescant in pace. ℟. Amen. Deinde dicto Placeat, datur benedictio, quae a Celebrante semper datur in Missa, praeterquam in Missis Defunctorum.

1. When the Canon and everything else up to the Communion are finished, and the Communion is made, the Communion and the Prayers after Communion are said in the same manner and order as at the beginning of Mass; and after the last, Dominus vobiscum. ℟. Et cum spiritu tuo is said again; and then Ite missa est or Benedicamus Domino, as the time requires. Ite missa est is said whenever the Gloria in excelsis has been said; when it has not, Benedicamus Domino is said in its place. ℟. Deo gratias. In Masses for the Dead, Requiescant in pace. ℟. Amen. Then, after the Placeat, the blessing is given, which the celebrant always gives at Mass, except in Masses for the Dead.

2. Deinde praemisso Dominus vobiscum, et ℟. Et cum spiritu tuo, legitur Evangelium S. Joannis In principio, praemisso Initium sancti Evangelii, etc. Notandum quod Evangelium In principio erat Verbum semper dicatur in fine cujuslibet Missae, nisi ad tertiam Missam Natalis Domini, loco cujus dicitur Evangelium Cum natus esset Jesus; et nisi in diebus ferialibus per totam Quadragesimam, quando Missa non est de Feria, excepta Missa conventuali, quia tunc dicitur Evangelium de Feria currente: et in Dominica Palmarum in Missis privatis legitur Evangelium, quod ponitur post Benedictionem palmarum: et quando in Dominicis de alio festo, quam de Dominicis celebratur; quia tunc in fine dicendum est Evangelium de Dominica, excepta Dominica quarta Adventus (si in eam incidat Vigilia Nativitatis Domini), cujus tunc in fine non legitur Evangelium; item ultimum Evangelium dicitur de Dominica in omnibus Missis, in quibus facta fuerit memoria Dominicae anticipatae; similiter in Feriis Quatuor Temporum Septembris, Adventus, et in diebus Rogationum, quando de festo aliquo celebratur, vel Missa votiva legitur, in fine dicitur Evangelium de Feria, nisi quando in Feria IV Quatuor Temporum Adventus dicitur Missa aliqua B. Mariae, quae habeat idem Evangelium ac illud dictae Feriae IV; in Vigiliis vero Sanctorum, quae occurrunt in Festis, vel Quatuor Temporibus, non legitur Evangelium Vigiliae in fine Missae. In Missis autem Defunctorum semper legitur Evangelium In principio.

2. Then, after Dominus vobiscum and ℟. Et cum spiritu tuo, the Gospel of St John In principio is read, preceded by Initium sancti Evangelii, etc. Note that the Gospel In principio erat Verbum is always said at the end of every Mass, except at the third Mass of Christmas, in place of which the Gospel Cum natus esset Jesus is said; and except on ferial days all through Lent when the Mass is not of the Feria (but not at the conventual Mass), for then the Gospel of the current Feria is said; and on Palm Sunday, in private Masses, the Gospel placed after the blessing of the palms is read; and when on Sundays the Mass is of a feast and not of the Sunday, for then the Gospel of the Sunday is to be said at the end, except on the fourth Sunday of Advent (if the Vigil of Christmas falls on it), whose Gospel is then not read at the end. The last Gospel is likewise of the Sunday in all Masses in which an anticipated Sunday has been commemorated; similarly, on the Ember days of September and Advent and on the Rogation days, when the Mass is of a feast or a votive Mass is read, the Gospel of the Feria is said at the end, except when on the Ember Wednesday of Advent a Mass of Our Lady is said which has the same Gospel as that Wednesday. On the Vigils of Saints that fall on feasts or Ember days, the Gospel of the Vigil is not read at the end of Mass. In Masses for the Dead the Gospel In principio is always read.

XVII De ordinanda Missa conformiter ad praedictas RubricasOf ordering the Mass according to these Rubrics

Habita supradictarum Rubricarum notitia, si celebraturus velit Missam diei ordinare, recurrat ad Ordinarium Missarum de Tempore, vel de Sanctis, secundum qualitatem temporis; si propriam non habeat, recurrat ad Commune Sanctorum. Si de Feria agendum sit, et propriam Missam non habuerit, dicatur Missa praecedentis Dominicae, omissis Gloria in excelsis et Credo, et uno Versu post Responsorium cum Alleluia: praeterquam tempore Paschali, in quo, in praefatis diebus, dicitur unicus Versus cum duobus Alleluia, ut supra notatum est. Si Missa votiva debeat dici, requiratur post Commune Sanctorum suo loco. Si plures Orationes dicendae sint, requirantur ante et post Proprium Sanctorum et post Missas votivas, suo loco, de singulis rebus singulae. Si pro Defunctis celebranda sit Missa, habetur post Missas votivas, cum Orationibus diversis circa finem Missalis.

Knowing these Rubrics, the priest who is to celebrate and wishes to order the Mass of the day should turn to the Masses of the Season or of the Saints, according to the time; if the Mass has no proper, to the Common of Saints. If the Feria is to be kept and has no Mass of its own, the Mass of the preceding Sunday is said, without the Gloria in excelsis and the Credo, and with one Verse with Alleluia after the Responsory; except in Paschaltide, when on those days a single Verse is said with two Alleluias, as noted above. If a votive Mass is to be said, it is found in its place after the Common of Saints. If several Prayers are to be said, they are found in their places, each for its subject, before and after the Proper of Saints and after the votive Masses. If Mass is to be celebrated for the Dead, it is found after the votive Masses, with the various Prayers, towards the end of the Missal.

XVIII De hora celebrandi MissamOf the hour of celebrating Mass

Missa privata quacumque hora diei ab aurora usque ad meridiem dici potest. Missa vero conventualis et major a festo Resurrectionis Domini usque ad festum Exaltationis sanctae Crucis exclusive dicatur post dictam a Choro Tertiam; praeterquam in sextis feriis, in Quatuor Temporibus infra Octavam Pentecostes, et in Vigiliis, in quibus jejunatur, quamvis sint dies solemnes, in quibus Missa debet cantari post Sextam. A festo autem Exaltationis S. Crucis usque ad diem Cinerum exclusive cantari semper debet post Sextam; et a die Cinerum usque ad Dominicam Resurrectionis cantanda est post Nonam, praeterquam in Dominicis, in quibus per totum annum cantatur Missa post Tertiam.

A private Mass may be said at any hour of the day from dawn until noon. The conventual and high Mass, from Easter to the feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross exclusive, is said after Terce has been said in Choir; except on Fridays, on the Ember days within the Octave of Pentecost and on fasting Vigils, even when they are solemn days, on which the Mass is to be sung after Sext. From the Exaltation of the Holy Cross to Ash Wednesday exclusive it is always to be sung after Sext; and from Ash Wednesday to Easter Sunday it is sung after None; except on Sundays, on which, all the year round, Mass is sung after Terce.

XIX De uniformitate servanda circa Missae celebrationemOf the uniformity to be kept in celebrating Mass

Caeremoniae omnes Ordinis nostri tam in altari, quam in choro, et in divino Officio conformiter et uniformiter ab omnibus observentur. Unde in Missa, tam privata, quam communi, nihil quod notatum non est in praefato nostro Ordinario interponatur, nec in principio, nec in fine: caveantque omnes, ne privato spiritu quidquam Missae, aut Officio nostro addant, detrahant, transponant, immutent, seu in verbis, seu in Ritu, secreto, aut clara voce.

All the ceremonies of our Order, at the altar, in choir and in the divine Office, are to be observed by all in conformity and uniformity. So in Mass, private or common, nothing is to be put in that is not marked in our Ordinarium, neither at the beginning nor at the end; and let all beware of adding to, taking from, transposing or changing anything in the Mass or our Office out of private spirit, whether in words or in rite, secretly or aloud.

XX De Altari, et aliis requisitis ad SacrificiumOf the altar and the other things required for the Sacrifice

1. Altare, in quo sacrosanctum Missae sacrificium celebrandum est, debet esse lapideum, et ab Episcopo, sive Abbate a sede Apostolica facultatem habente consecratum: vel saltem ara lapidea perpolita (quae vocatur altare portatile) similiter ab Episcopo, vel Abbate, ut supra, consecrata, integra, aut non pro majori parte fracta; sed tam ampla sit, ut hostiam, et majorem partem calicis capiat: quae quidem mensae altaris apte inseratur, atque ita, ut infra utrumque mensae cornu in medio collocata ab ipsius fronte non longius distet spatio circiter palmi. Sic vero illa inserta sit altari, ut e mensae superficie paululum aliquid exstet, quo Celebrantis tactu dignosci possit.

1. The altar on which the most holy Sacrifice of the Mass is celebrated must be of stone and consecrated by a Bishop, or by an Abbot having the faculty from the Apostolic See; or at least there must be a polished altar-stone (called a portable altar), likewise consecrated by a Bishop or Abbot as above, whole, or not broken in its greater part, and large enough to hold the host and the greater part of the chalice. It is to be fitly let into the table of the altar, placed in the middle between the two horns, not more than about a palm's breadth from the front; and so set into the altar that it stands a little above the surface of the table, so that the celebrant can tell it by touch.

2. Altaris vero mensa, maxime consecrata, decenter ornata et munda sit, tribusque mappis lineis et mundis, ab Episcopo, vel ab alio habente potestatem benedictis strata, superiori saltem oblonga, quae usque ad terram pertingat, duabus aliis brevioribus, vel una duplicata; quae etiam habeant coopertorium coriaceum, vel ex grossiori et colorata tela, quod a pulvere, et ab aliis immunditiis ipsas mappas praeservet.

2. The table of the altar, above all a consecrated one, is to be decently adorned and clean, and covered with three clean linen cloths blessed by a Bishop or another who has the power, the uppermost at least long enough to reach to the ground, the other two shorter, or one folded double; and they are to have a cover of leather or of coarser coloured cloth to keep the cloths from dust and other dirt.

3. Super altare collocetur crux Christi crucifixi in medio, et candelabra saltem duo cum candelis accensis hinc et inde, in utroque ejus latere: et ad pedem crucis ponatur tabella, continens formas consecrationis.

3. On the altar the cross of Christ crucified is set in the middle, and at least two candlesticks with lighted candles, one on either side of it; and at the foot of the cross is placed a card containing the words of consecration.

4. In cornu Epistolae pulvinar seu cussinus supponendus Missali apponatur; et ab eadem parte ponatur cereus ad consecrationem et elevationem Sacramenti accendendus, et teneatur a ministro accensus usque ad communionem peractam.

4. At the Epistle horn a pillow or cushion is placed to set the Missal on; and on the same side a candle is put, to be lit at the consecration and elevation of the Sacrament, and kept lit by the server until the communion is done.

5. Sit etiam a cornu Epistolae parva campanula, qua aliquot ictibus admoneatur populus ante elevationem.

5. At the Epistle horn there is also to be a small bell, with which the people are warned by a few strokes before the elevation.

6. Ampullae vini et aquae, non ex aurichalco, aliove impuro metalli genere, sed ex argento, aut stanno, vel potius e crystallo, aut vitro perlucido: ab omni etiam parte sordibus purae, et suo quaeque operculo stanneo, argenteove apte contectae sint: et cum pelvicula, et manutergio mundo ab eodem cornu in fenestella, seu in parva mensa ad hoc praeparata collocentur. Super altare autem, vel sub ipso nihil omnino ponatur, seu recondatur, quod ad Missae sacrificium, vel ad ipsius altaris ornatum non pertineat.

6. The cruets of wine and water are not to be of brass or another base metal, but of silver or pewter, or better of crystal or clear glass; clean of all dirt, each fitly closed with its lid of pewter or silver; and with a small basin and a clean towel they are set on the same side in a niche or on a small table prepared for it. Nothing at all is to be put on the altar or kept under it that does not belong to the Sacrifice of the Mass or to the adornment of the altar itself.

7. Altarium mappae, nec non et strophiola, ac alia ad usum altarium et Sacerdotum deputata saepe laventur: sintque purificatoria alba, et bene nitida.

7. The altar cloths, the small towels and the other things set apart for the use of the altars and of the priests are to be washed often; and the purificators are to be white and very clean.

8. Corporalia e tela linea, pura et candida sint, simplicia, nihil elaborata, nulla in parte rupta, et nihil serici intextum habeant; acus opere simplici punctim solum retorta sint. Eadem non notabiliter parva, aut magna erunt, sed convenientis mensurae, ita ut latitudo sit, quae quatuor plicas in longum, et tres in latum non excedat.

8. Corporals are to be of linen, pure and white, plain and unworked, torn in no part, and with no silk woven in; they may only be hemmed with simple needlework. They are not to be noticeably small or large, but of a fitting size, so that they do not exceed four folds in length and three in width.

9. In bursis vero (quae ita vocantur) reponantur: illaeque intrinsecus serico, aut tela tenui candida circumvestitae sint: et illae super velum calicis imponantur, dum calix ad altare deferendus est, habeantque crucem, vel aliam sanctam imaginem ex una parte intextam.

9. They are kept in burses (as they are called), lined inside with silk or fine white cloth; the burse is laid on the chalice veil when the chalice is carried to the altar, and has a cross or another holy image worked on one side.

De Coloribus quibus utendum est in ecclesiasticis indumentis, et paramentis altarisOf the colours to be used in the church vestments and the altar hangings

Indumenta Celebrantis et ministrorum, et paramenta altaris debent esse coloris convenientis Officio et Missae diei secundum Ritum nostrum, in quo quinque coloribus uti consuevimus, Albo, Rubeo, Viridi, Violaceo, et Nigro.

The vestments of the celebrant and ministers and the hangings of the altar must be of the colour that suits the Office and Mass of the day according to our Rite, in which we are accustomed to use five colours: white, red, green, violet and black.

De albo. 1. Colore albo utimur a Vesperis Vigiliae Natalis Domini usque ad Octavam Epiphaniae inclusive, praeterquam in festis sanctorum Martyrum, quae illo tempore interveniunt: Feria quinta in Cena Domini ad Missam, et Evangelium post Mandatum: In Sabbato sancto ad benedictionem Cerei, et ad Officium Missae; et deinceps usque ad Missam in Vigilia Pentecostes exclusive, quando de Tempore agitur, praeterquam in Missa Rogationum: In festo Sanctissimae Trinitatis: In festo SS. Corporis Christi: In festo SS. Cordis Jesu: In festo Transfigurationis Domini: In festo D. N. J. C. Regis: In omnibus festis B. Mariae Virginis, etiam in benedictione Candelarum, et Processione in Purificatione: In omnibus festis Angelorum: In Nativitate sancti Joannis Baptistae: In Conversione sancti Pauli Apostoli: In utraque Cathedra S. Petri, et in festo ejusdem ad Vincula: In festis sanctorum Doctorum Ecclesiae, et Confessorum Pontificum, et non Pontificum: In festis sancti Dominici Patris nostri, et omnium Sanctorum Ordinis, non Martyrum: In omnibus festis sanctarum Virginum, et non Virginum, nec Martyrum: In festo Omnium Sanctorum: In principali festo sancti Joannis Evangelistae: In Dedicatione, et Consecratione Ecclesiae, vel Altaris: In Consecratione summi Pontificis, et Coronatione: Item per Octavas praedictorum festorum, quando fit Missa de Octava, et in Dominicis infra Octavas solemnissimas occurrentibus, quando in eis fit Officium de Dominica: In Missis votivis supradictorum festorum, quocumque tempore dicantur: In Missa pro Sponso et Sponsa.

White. 1. We use white from Vespers of the Vigil of Christmas to the Octave of the Epiphany inclusive, except on the feasts of holy Martyrs that fall in that time; on Maundy Thursday at Mass, and for the Gospel after the Mandatum; on Holy Saturday at the blessing of the Candle and for the Office of the Mass, and from then until the Mass of the Vigil of Pentecost exclusive, when the Office is of the Season, except at the Mass of the Rogations; on the feasts of the Holy Trinity, Corpus Christi, the Sacred Heart, the Transfiguration and Christ the King; on all feasts of the Blessed Virgin Mary, also at the blessing of Candles and the Procession on the Purification; on all feasts of the Angels; on the Nativity of St John the Baptist; on the Conversion of St Paul the Apostle; on both feasts of the Chair of St Peter and on his Chains; on the feasts of holy Doctors of the Church and of Confessors, Bishops and not Bishops; on the feasts of our Father St Dominic and of all the Saints of the Order who are not Martyrs; on all feasts of holy Virgins, and of those neither Virgins nor Martyrs; on All Saints; on the principal feast of St John the Evangelist; at the Dedication and Consecration of a church or altar; at the Consecration and Coronation of the Supreme Pontiff; likewise through the Octaves of these feasts when the Mass is of the Octave, and on Sundays within the most solemn Octaves when the Office is of the Sunday; in the votive Masses of these feasts, at whatever time they are said; and in the Mass for Bride and Bridegroom.

De rubeo. 2. Colore rubeo utimur a Missa Vigiliae Pentecostes usque ad Sabbatum sequens inclusive: In utroque festo sanctae Crucis: In Decollatione sancti Joannis Baptistae: In omnibus festis Apostolorum, et Evangelistarum: praeterquam in principali festo S. Joannis Evangelistae, et festis Conversionis S. Pauli, Cathedrarum S. Petri, et ad Vincula ejusdem: In festo S. Joannis ante portam Latinam: In Commemoratione S. Pauli Apostoli: In omnibus festis sanctorum Martyrum, et SS. Virginum Martyrum, et Martyrum non Virginum: In festo SS. Innocentium, quando in Dominica evenerit: in ejus vero die octava semper utimur rubeo, quocumque die occurrat: In festo Coronae Domini: In festo Pretiosissimi Sanguinis: Item in Octavis praedictorum festorum, quando fit de Octava. In Missis votivis supradictorum festorum, et in Missa pro eligendo summo Pontifice.

Red. 2. We use red from the Mass of the Vigil of Pentecost to the following Saturday inclusive; on both feasts of the Holy Cross; on the Beheading of St John the Baptist; on all feasts of the Apostles and Evangelists, except the principal feast of St John the Evangelist and the feasts of the Conversion of St Paul, the Chairs of St Peter and his Chains; on the feast of St John before the Latin Gate; on the Commemoration of St Paul the Apostle; on all feasts of holy Martyrs, of holy Virgin Martyrs and of Martyrs not Virgins; on the feast of the Holy Innocents when it falls on a Sunday, while on its Octave day we always use red, on whatever day it falls; on the feasts of the Crown of our Lord and of the Most Precious Blood; likewise in the Octaves of these feasts when the Office is of the Octave; in the votive Masses of these feasts, and in the Mass for the election of a Supreme Pontiff.

De viridi. 3. Colore viridi utimur ab Octava Epiphaniae exclusive usque ad Septuagesimam exclusive, et a prima Dominica post Octavam SS. Cordis Jesu usque ad Adventum exclusive, in Missis et Officiis de Tempore: Item in Missa de Dominica, quae infra hebdomadam resumitur.

Green. 3. We use green from the Octave of the Epiphany exclusive to Septuagesima exclusive, and from the first Sunday after the Octave of the Sacred Heart to Advent exclusive, in the Masses and Offices of the Season; likewise in the Mass of a Sunday resumed during the week.

De violaceo. 4. Colore violaceo utimur a prima Dominica Adventus usque ad Vigiliam Nativitatis Domini in Missa inclusive, in Missis de Tempore, et a Septuagesima usque ad Cenam Domini exclusive, in Officio de Tempore, etiam in Missa Dominicae infra hebdomadam resumpta. In Missis Litaniarum, Rogationum, Quatuor Temporum, et Vigiliarum, in quibus jejunatur, exceptis Vigilia et Quatuor Temporibus Pentecostes: In festo SS. Innocentium, extra Dominicam: In Missis votivis de Passione Domini, Pro quacumque necessitate, Pro peccatis, Ad tollendum schisma, Contra paganos, Tempore belli, Pro pace, Pro vitanda mortalitate, Pro iter agentibus, Pro infirmis, et pro ceteris hujusmodi.

Violet. 4. We use violet from the first Sunday of Advent to the Mass of the Vigil of Christmas inclusive, in the Masses of the Season, and from Septuagesima to Maundy Thursday exclusive, in the Office of the Season, also in the Mass of a Sunday resumed during the week; in the Masses of the Litanies, the Rogations, the Ember days and the fasting Vigils, except the Vigil and Ember days of Pentecost; on the feast of the Holy Innocents, outside a Sunday; in the votive Masses of the Passion of our Lord, For any necessity, For sins, For ending a schism, Against the heathen, In time of war, For peace, For averting mortality, For travellers, For the sick, and the like.

De nigro. 5. Colore nigro utimur Feria sexta in Parasceve, et in omnibus Missis et exsequiis Defunctorum.

Black. 5. We use black on Good Friday, and in all Masses and funerals for the Dead.

In festis solemnissimis. 6. In diebus vero solemnissimis, idest, qui habent Octavas solemnissimas, vel solemnes, uti possumus pretiosioribus paramentis, cujuscumque sint coloris, dummodo non sint nigri.

On the most solemn feasts. 6. On the most solemn days, that is, those which have most solemn or solemn Octaves, we may use the more precious vestments, of whatever colour, provided they are not black.

In processionibus. 7. In Processionibus quibuscumque utimur eodem colore, quo etiam utimur eo die ad Missas, ut supra: nisi in Processione de SS. Sacramento, in qua utimur albo, sicuti in benedictione Palmarum, et Processione.

In processions. 7. In all processions we use the same colour as at the Masses that day, as above; except in the Procession of the Blessed Sacrament, in which we use white, as at the blessing of the Palms and the Procession.

De qualitate indumentorum. 1. Celebraturus Missam semper utitur manipulo, stola, et casula, seu planeta super albam.

Of the kinds of vestments. 1. The priest who is to celebrate Mass always wears the maniple, stole and chasuble, or planeta, over the alb.

2. Cappa, seu pluviali utitur Sacerdos in Processionibus, et Benedictionibus, quae fiunt in altari: Item in Officio Laudum, et Vesperarum, quando solemniter dicuntur: Item quando Sacerdos post Missam Defunctorum facit commendationes in sepultura Defuncti, vel etiam circa feretrum. Dum autem utitur cappa, semper deponat manipulum.

2. The priest uses the cope, or pluvial, in processions and in blessings given at the altar; likewise at Lauds and Vespers when they are said solemnly; likewise when after a Mass for the Dead he makes the commendations at the burial of the deceased, or around the bier. When he wears the cope he always lays aside the maniple.

3. Dalmatica, seu tunicella in Missis conventualibus utuntur Diaconus et Subdiaconus, omnibus Dominicis diebus, et in festis trium Lectionum, et majoribus: Item in omnibus Missis votivis, quae singulis septimanis in Ordine nostro ex privilegio, vel consuetudine, secundum morem Provinciarum dici solent; et similiter in omnibus Octavis, de quibus fit Officium.

3. The deacon and subdeacon wear the dalmatic, or tunicle, at conventual Masses on all Sundays, on feasts of three Lessons and greater ones; likewise in all the votive Masses customarily said each week in our Order by privilege or custom, according to the usage of the Provinces; and likewise in all Octaves whose Office is said.

4. Tempore vero Feriarum Quadragesimae, et in quibuscumque aliis Missis de Feria, Vigiliarum, Rogationum, et Quatuor Temporum Septembris, et Adventus (nisi aliquod festum illis diebus occurrerit), alba tantum vestiti ministrant Subdiaconus cum manipulo, et Diaconus, ultra manipulum, cum stola, ab humero sinistro pendente sub dexterum.

4. On the Ferias of Lent, and in any other Masses of a Feria, of Vigils, of the Rogations and of the Ember days of September and Advent (unless a feast falls on those days), the ministers serve vested only in the alb: the subdeacon with the maniple, and the deacon, besides the maniple, with the stole hanging from the left shoulder to under the right.

5. Dum autem resumitur Missa de Dominica infra hebdomadam (quia fuit impedita), servetur qualitas diei Officii pro usu dalmaticae.

5. When the Mass of a Sunday is resumed during the week (because it was impeded), the character of the day's Office is followed for the use of the dalmatic.

6. Item in obitu, et exsequiis Fratris defuncti, vel alterius personae, et in Missis Anniversarii Ordinis, vel nobilis aut excellentis personae, dalmatica utendum est. In aliis autem Missis pro Defunctis servetur ritus, et modus Officii diei, de quo Missa conventualis dicitur, vel dicenda foret, nisi occurreret pro Defunctis celebrandum.

6. Likewise at the death and funeral of a deceased brother or of another person, and in the Masses of the Anniversaries of the Order or of a noble or eminent person, the dalmatic is to be used. In other Masses for the Dead the rite and manner of the day's Office are followed, of which the conventual Mass is said, or would be said if Mass did not have to be celebrated for the Dead.
