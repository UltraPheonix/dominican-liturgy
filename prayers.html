Orationes dicendae cum Sacerdos induitur sacris vestibusPrayers said as the priest puts on the sacred vestments

Ad lotionem manuumAt the washing of the hands

Sacerdos, cum lavat manus, dicat:

The priest, as he washes his hands, says:

Da, Domine, virtutem manibus meis ad abstergendam omnem maculam, ut sine pollutione mentis et corporis valeam tibi servire. Amen.

Give virtue to my hands, O Lord, to wipe away every stain, that I may be able to serve thee without defilement of mind or body. Amen.

Ante AmictumBefore the amice

Priusquam amictum capiti imponat, Sacerdos se signat signo Crucis, dicens:

Before putting the amice on his head, the priest signs himself with the sign of the cross, saying:

In nomine Patris, et Filii, et Spiritus Sancti. Amen.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.

Ad AmictumAt the amice

Dum ponitur super caput, dicat:

As it is put on the head, he says:

Impone, Domine, capiti meo galeam salutis, ad expugnandos omnes diabolicos incursus. Amen.

Place upon my head, O Lord, the helmet of salvation, for overcoming all the assaults of the devil. Amen.

Ad AlbamAt the alb

Cum ea induitur:

As he puts it on:

Dealba me, Domine, et munda cor meum, ut in Sanguine Agni dealbatus, gaudiis perfruar sempiternis. Amen.

Make me white, O Lord, and cleanse my heart, that, made white in the Blood of the Lamb, I may enjoy everlasting gladness. Amen.

Ad CingulumAt the girdle

Dum se cingit:

As he girds himself:

Praecinge me, Domine, cingulo puritatis, et exstingue in lumbis meis humorem libidinis; ut maneat in me virtus continentiae, et castitatis. Amen.

Gird me, O Lord, with the girdle of purity, and quench in my loins the heat of lust, that the virtue of continence and chastity may abide in me. Amen.

Ad ManipulumAt the maniple

Dum imponitur brachio sinistro:

As it is put on the left arm:

Merear, Domine, portare manipulum fletus et doloris, ut cum exsultatione recipiam mercedem laboris. Amen.

May I be worthy, O Lord, to bear the maniple of weeping and sorrow, that with rejoicing I may receive the reward of my labour. Amen.

Ad StolamAt the stole

Dum imponitur collo:

As it is put about the neck:

Redde mihi, Domine, obsecro, stolam immortalitatis, quam perdidi in praevaricatione primi parentis: et quamvis indignus accedo ad tuum sacrum mysterium, merear tamen gaudium sempiternum. Amen.

Give back to me, O Lord, I beseech thee, the stole of immortality which I lost through the transgression of our first parent; and though I unworthily approach thy sacred mystery, may I yet be worthy of everlasting joy. Amen.

Ad CasulamAt the chasuble

Cum assumitur:

As it is put on:

Domine, qui dixisti: Jugum meum suave est, et onus meum leve: fac, ut istud portare sic valeam, quod consequar tuam gratiam. Amen.

O Lord, who hast said: My yoke is sweet and my burden light; grant that I may so bear it as to obtain thy grace. Amen.

Orationes ante et post Missam pro opportunitate Sacerdotis dicendae.

Prayers before and after Mass, to be said as the priest has opportunity.

De dignitate Sacerdotis, ex S. AugustinoOf the dignity of the priest, from St Augustine

Veneranda Sacerdotum dignitas, in quorum manibus Dei Filius, velut in utero Virginis, incarnatur! O felices Sacerdotes, si sacerdotaliter vixeritis! O caeleste mysterium, quod per vos Pater, et Filius, et Spiritus Sanctus tam mirabiliter operantur, sub tam ineffabili ministerio vestro: quod uno eodemque momento idem Deus, qui praesidet in caelo, in manibus vestris est in sacrificio! Caelum miratur, horrescit infernus, contremiscit diabolus, reveretur quamplurimum Angelica celsitudo.

Venerable is the dignity of priests, in whose hands the Son of God becomes incarnate as in the Virgin's womb! O happy priests, if you live as priests! O heavenly mystery, which the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost so wonderfully work through you, by so ineffable a ministry of yours: that in one and the same moment the same God who reigns in heaven is in your hands in the sacrifice! Heaven marvels, hell shudders, the devil trembles, the height of the Angels shows the deepest reverence.

Quid retribuam Domino (ut taceam de ceteris) pro his, quae retribuit mihi, pro tam singulari dono sacrificii mihi impenso, quod est datum optimum, et omne donum perfectum? O venerabilium sanctitudo manuum! O felix exercitium, ubi tractat Sacerdos Christum Dei Filium, cujus deliciae sunt esse cum filiis hominum! O Sacerdotes! attendite: major est dignitas vobis collata, quam Angelis, qui adorant quod vos conficitis, quod nec ipsi conficere possunt. O altitudo sapientiae Dei! O ineffabilis clementia Salvatoris! Quod datum non est Angelis, concessum est homini. Sacerdos hoc Sacramentum ineffabile conficit: Angelus conficienti, quasi famulus, assistit.

What shall I render to the Lord (to say nothing of the rest) for the things he has rendered to me, for so singular a gift of the sacrifice bestowed on me, which is the best gift and every perfect gift? O holiness of venerable hands! O happy task, in which the priest handles Christ the Son of God, whose delight is to be with the children of men! O priests, take heed: a greater dignity is given to you than to the Angels, who adore what you make present, which they themselves cannot. O depth of the wisdom of God! O ineffable mercy of the Saviour! What was not given to the Angels has been granted to man. The priest confects this ineffable Sacrament; the Angel stands by him as he does so, like a servant.

O quam venerabile, quam gloriosum, quam insigne fuit apud antiquos nomen Sacerdotis! Sed hodie quid abjectius, aut quid vilius? O Sacerdotes! si anima cujuslibet justi sedes Dei est; multo magis sedes, et templum Dei esse debetis mundum et immaculatum. Si beatus est venter, qui novem mensibus Christum portavit, ita, et si non tantum, tamen beata debent esse corda vestra, in quibus quotidie eligit sibi hospitium Filius Dei. Si beata sunt ubera, quae parvulus suxit; ita debet ille esse beatus, qui Carnem ejus sumit, et Sanguinem sugit. Carnes ergo vestras cum timore ejus configite; et vobis diligenter providete, ne lingua, quae vocat de caelo Filium Dei, contra Dominum loquatur; et ne manus, quae intinguntur Sanguine Christi, polluantur sanguine peccati.

How venerable, how glorious, how illustrious was the name of priest among the ancients! But today what is more despised, or what more worthless? O priests! if the soul of every just man is the seat of God, much more ought you to be a seat and temple of God, clean and spotless. If blessed is the womb that bore Christ nine months, so too, if not as much, yet blessed ought your hearts to be, in which the Son of God daily chooses his lodging. If blessed are the breasts which the infant sucked, so must he be blessed who takes his Flesh and drinks his Blood. Pierce your flesh therefore with his fear, and take careful heed that the tongue which calls the Son of God down from heaven speak not against the Lord, and that the hands which are steeped in the Blood of Christ be not defiled with the blood of sin.

Quam graviter peccat sacerdos suscipiens Corpus Christi in peccato, ex SS. Ambrosio et AugustinoHow gravely the priest sins who receives the Body of Christ in sin, from St Ambrose and St Augustine

Sanctus Ambrosius: Qui indigne Christum sumit, idem est, ac si eum interficiat. Et sanctus Augustinus: Magis peccant, qui Christum tradunt peccantibus membris, quam qui tradiderunt eum Judaeis crucifigendum. Sicut enim Judas tradidit Christum indigne persecutoribus; sic sumens Corpus Christi indigne, judicium sibi manducat, et bibit, non dijudicans Corpus Domini, ut inquit Apostolus; et talis tradit Christum, quantum in se est, daemonibus, dum ponit eum in loco, qui est in potestate daemonum.

St Ambrose: Whoever receives Christ unworthily, it is the same as if he killed him. And St Augustine: They sin more who deliver Christ to sinful members than those who delivered him to the Jews to be crucified. For as Judas delivered Christ unworthily to his persecutors, so he who receives the Body of Christ unworthily eats and drinks judgement to himself, not discerning the Body of the Lord, as the Apostle says; and such a one, as far as in him lies, delivers Christ to the demons, putting him in a place which is in the power of the demons.

Malus enim Sacerdos similis est ipsis Dathan et Abiron, quos divina ultio praecipitavit in infernum. Similiter Nadab et Abiu in thuribulo ignem posuerunt alienum, et ab igne consumpti sunt; sic mali Sacerdotes ignem luxuriae et cupiditatis in sui cordis thuribulo ponentes, ab igne infernali finaliter succendentur. Mali Sacerdotes etiam sunt Antiocho similes, qui templum Domini violavit, et vasa templi in usus profanos convertit; tandem vermibus consumptus pessima morte interiit. Sic mali Sacerdotes templum Domini, id est, conscientias proprias (1 ad Cor. 3: Templum Dei sanctum est, quod estis vos), violant, malum exemplum dando laicis. Incendunt per luxuriam, spoliant per virtutum expulsionem; et vasa templi, id est, intellectum, memoriam, et voluntatem convertunt in profanos usus, dum his abutuntur; et in peccatorum abyssum seipsos praecipitant. Quid Antiocho acciderit, saepe considerare debent.

For a wicked priest is like Dathan and Abiron, whom divine vengeance cast down into hell. Likewise Nadab and Abiu put strange fire in the censer and were consumed by fire; so wicked priests, putting the fire of lust and greed in the censer of their heart, will at last be set ablaze by the fire of hell. Wicked priests are also like Antiochus, who profaned the temple of the Lord and turned the vessels of the temple to profane uses, and at last, eaten by worms, died a most wretched death. So wicked priests profane the temple of the Lord, that is, their own consciences (1 Cor. 3: The temple of God is holy, which you are), giving a bad example to the laity. They set it on fire by lust, they plunder it by driving out the virtues; and they turn the vessels of the temple, that is the understanding, the memory and the will, to profane uses by abusing them; and they cast themselves into the abyss of sins. They should often consider what befell Antiochus.

Oratio ante Confessionem sacramentalemPrayer before sacramental Confession

Suscipe confessionem meam, piissime ac clementissime Domine Jesu Christe, unica spes salutis meae; et da mihi, obsecro, contritionem cordis, et lacrimas oculis meis, ut defleam diebus ac noctibus omnes negligentias meas, cum humilitate, et puritate cordis. Appropinquet oratio mea in conspectu tuo, Domine. Si iratus fueris contra me, quem adjutorem quaeram? Quis miserebitur iniquitatibus meis? Memento, Domine, quod Chananaeam et Publicanum vocasti ad poenitentiam, et Petrum lacrymantem suscepisti. Domine Deus meus, suscipe preces meas.

Receive my confession, most loving and most merciful Lord Jesus Christ, only hope of my salvation; and give me, I beseech thee, contrition of heart and tears for my eyes, that I may weep day and night for all my negligences, with humility and purity of heart. Let my prayer come near before thee, O Lord. If thou art angry with me, what helper shall I seek? Who will have pity on my iniquities? Remember, O Lord, that thou didst call the woman of Canaan and the publican to repentance, and didst receive Peter in his tears. O Lord my God, receive my prayers.

Salvator mundi, Jesu bone, qui te crucis morti dedisti, ut peccatores salvares, respice me miserum invocantem nomen tuum. Et si commisi, unde me damnare potes; tu non amisisti, unde salvare soles. Parce ergo mihi, qui es Salvator meus, et miserere peccatrici animae meae. Solve vincula ejus, sana vulnera ejus. Domine Jesu, te desidero, te quaero, te volo: ostende mihi faciem tuam, et salvus ero. Mitte igitur, piissime Domine, meritis purissimae et immaculatae semper Virginis Mariae, lucem tuam et veritatem tuam in animam meam, quae omnes defectus meos in veritate mihi ostendat, quos confiteri me oportet; atque juvet et doceat ipsos plene, et cum debita contritione explicare. Qui vivis et regnas Deus, per omnia saecula saeculorum. Amen.

Saviour of the world, good Jesus, who didst give thyself to the death of the cross to save sinners, look upon me, a wretch calling upon thy name. And if I have done that for which thou canst condemn me, thou hast not lost that by which thou art wont to save. Spare me then, who art my Saviour, and have mercy on my sinful soul. Loose its bonds, heal its wounds. Lord Jesus, I long for thee, I seek thee, I want thee: show me thy face, and I shall be saved. Send then, most loving Lord, by the merits of the most pure and immaculate ever-Virgin Mary, thy light and thy truth into my soul, to show me truly all my faults which I must confess, and to help and teach me to declare them fully and with due contrition. Who livest and reignest God, world without end. Amen.

Post poenitentiamAfter the penance

Peracta poenitentia per confessarium imposita, dicat sequentem orationem:

Having done the penance given by the confessor, he says the following prayer:

Sit tibi, Domine, obsecro, meritis sanctissimae Matris tuae semper Virginis Mariae, grata et accepta ista confessio mea: et quidquid mihi defuit, nunc et alias, de sufficientia contritionis, de puritate et integritate confessionis, suppleat pietas, et misericordia tua, quae dignetur me habere plenius et perfectius absolutum in caelo. Qui vivis et regnas Deus, per omnia saecula saeculorum. Amen.

May this my confession, O Lord, I beseech thee, by the merits of thy most holy Mother, the ever-Virgin Mary, be pleasing and acceptable to thee; and whatever has been wanting in me, now and at other times, in sufficiency of contrition, in purity and completeness of confession, may thy goodness and mercy supply, and vouchsafe to hold me more fully and perfectly absolved in heaven. Who livest and reignest God, world without end. Amen.

Oratio S. Thomae de Aquino ad Deum Patrem, dum Sacerdos est accessurus ad altarePrayer of St Thomas Aquinas to God the Father, when the priest is about to go to the altar

Omnipotens sempiterne Deus, ecce accedo ad sacramentum unigeniti Filii tui Domini nostri Jesu Christi. Accedo tamquam infirmus ad medicum vitae, immundus ad fontem misericordiae, caecus ad lumen claritatis aeternae, pauper et egenus ad Dominum caeli et terrae. Rogo ergo immensae largitatis tuae abundantiam, quatenus meam curare digneris infirmitatem, lavare foeditatem, illuminare caecitatem, ditare paupertatem, vestire nuditatem: ut panem Angelorum, Regem regum, et Dominum dominantium tanta suscipiam reverentia et humilitate, tanta contritione et devotione, tanta puritate et fide, tali proposito et intentione, sicut expedit saluti animae meae.

Almighty, everlasting God, behold I come to the sacrament of thine only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ. I come as one sick to the physician of life, as one unclean to the fountain of mercy, as one blind to the light of eternal brightness, as one poor and needy to the Lord of heaven and earth. I ask therefore the abundance of thy boundless bounty, that thou vouchsafe to heal my sickness, to wash my uncleanness, to enlighten my blindness, to enrich my poverty, to clothe my nakedness; that I may receive the bread of Angels, the King of kings and Lord of lords, with such reverence and humility, such contrition and devotion, such purity and faith, such purpose and intention, as is profitable to the salvation of my soul.

Da mihi, quaeso, Dominici Corporis et Sanguinis non solum suscipere sacramentum, sed etiam rem et virtutem sacramenti. O mitissime Deus, da mihi Corpus unigeniti Filii tui Domini nostri Jesu Christi, quod traxit de Virgine Maria, sic suscipere, ut corpori suo mystico merear incorporari, et inter ejus membra connumerari. O amantissime Pater, concede mihi dilectum Filium tuum, quem nunc velatum in via suscipere propono, revelata tandem facie perpetuo contemplari. Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitate Spiritus Sancti Deus, per omnia saecula saeculorum. Amen.

Grant me, I pray, to receive not only the sacrament of the Body and Blood of the Lord, but also the reality and power of the sacrament. O most gentle God, grant me so to receive the Body of thine only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, which he took from the Virgin Mary, that I may be worthy to be incorporated into his mystical body and numbered among his members. O most loving Father, grant me at last to behold for ever, with face unveiled, thy beloved Son, whom now on the way I purpose to receive under a veil. Who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world without end. Amen.

Ad Sanctum, vel Sanctam, cujus ea die festum celebraturTo the Saint whose feast is kept that day

O sancte N. (vel sancta N.), ecce ego miser peccator, de tuis meritis confisus, offero nunc Deo sacratissimum sacramentum Corporis et Sanguinis Domini nostri Jesu Christi pro tuo honore et gloria: precor te humiliter et devote, ut pro me hodie intercedere digneris; ut tantum sacrificium digne et acceptabiliter offerre valeam, et eum tecum, et cum omnibus electis ejus aeternaliter laudare, et cum eo regnare valeam. Amen.

O holy N., behold, I, a wretched sinner, trusting in thy merits, now offer to God the most sacred sacrament of the Body and Blood of our Lord Jesus Christ for thy honour and glory: I pray thee humbly and devoutly to vouchsafe to intercede for me today, that I may offer so great a sacrifice worthily and acceptably, and may praise him for ever with thee and with all his elect, and reign with him. Amen.

Oratio sancti Ambrosii EpiscopiPrayer of St Ambrose, Bishop

Sacerdos in Missa conventuali, dum Kyrie eleison, vel Gloria in excelsis Deo, cantatur in choro, potest, si vult, dicere sequentem Orationem, divisam per singulas Hebdomadae dies.

At the conventual Mass, while the Kyrie eleison or the Gloria in excelsis Deo is sung in choir, the priest may, if he wishes, say the following prayer, divided among the days of the week.

Die Dominica Summe Sacerdos, et vere Pontifex Jesu Christe, qui te obtulisti Deo Patri hostiam puram et immaculatam in ara Crucis pro nobis miseris peccatoribus, et qui dedisti nobis Carnem tuam ad manducandum, et Sanguinem tuum ad bibendum; et posuisti mysterium istud in virtute Spiritus Sancti, dicens: Haec quotiescumque feceritis, in mei memoriam facietis: rogo per eumdem Sanguinem tuum, magnum salutis nostrae pretium; rogo per hanc mirabilem et ineffabilem caritatem, qua nos miseros et indignos sic amare dignatus es, ut lavares nos a peccatis nostris in Sanguine tuo: doce me indignum servum tuum, quem, inter cetera dona tua, etiam ad officium Sacerdotale vocare dignatus es, nullis meis meritis, sed sola dignatione misericordiae tuae; doce me, quaeso, per Spiritum Sanctum tuum, tantum tractare mysterium ea reverentia et honore, ea devotione et timore, quibus oportet et decet.

Sunday High Priest and true Pontiff, Jesus Christ, who didst offer thyself to God the Father, a pure and spotless victim, on the altar of the Cross for us wretched sinners, and didst give us thy Flesh to eat and thy Blood to drink, and didst establish this mystery in the power of the Holy Ghost, saying: As often as ye shall do these things, ye shall do them in remembrance of me: I pray thee by that same Blood, the great price of our salvation; I pray thee by that wonderful and ineffable love with which thou didst vouchsafe so to love us, wretched and unworthy, as to wash us from our sins in thy Blood: teach me, thine unworthy servant, whom among thy other gifts thou hast vouchsafed to call also to the priestly office, through no merits of mine but by the sole condescension of thy mercy; teach me, I pray, by thy Holy Spirit, to handle so great a mystery with the reverence and honour, the devotion and fear, that are due and fitting.

Fac me per gratiam tuam semper illud de tanto mysterio credere et intelligere, sentire et firmiter tenere, dicere et cogitare, quod tibi placet, et expedit animae meae. Intret spiritus tuus bonus in cor meum, qui sonet ibi sine sono, et sine strepitu verborum loquatur omnem veritatem mysteriorum. Profunda sunt quippe nimis, et sacro tecta velamine. Propter magnam clementiam tuam concede mihi Missarum solemnia mundo corde, et pura mente celebrare. Libera cor meum ab immundis et nefandis, vanis et noxiis cogitationibus. Muni me beatorum Angelorum pia et fida custodia, ac tutela fortissima, ut hostes omnium bonorum confusi discedant. Per virtutem tanti mysterii, et per manum sancti Angeli tui repelle a me, et a cunctis servis tuis durissimum spiritum superbiae et cenodoxiae, invidiae et blasphemiae, fornicationis et immunditiae, dubietatis et diffidentiae. Confundantur, qui nos persequuntur: pereant illi, qui nos perdere festinant.

Make me by thy grace always believe and understand, feel and firmly hold, speak and think of so great a mystery what pleases thee and is good for my soul. Let thy good spirit enter my heart, to sound there without sound, and without the noise of words to speak all the truth of the mysteries. For they are exceeding deep, and covered with a sacred veil. Of thy great mercy grant me to celebrate the solemnities of the Mass with a clean heart and a pure mind. Free my heart from unclean and wicked, vain and harmful thoughts. Fortify me with the loving and faithful keeping and the strong protection of the blessed Angels, that the enemies of all good may depart confounded. By the power of so great a mystery, and by the hand of thy holy Angel, drive away from me and from all thy servants the hard spirit of pride and vainglory, of envy and blasphemy, of fornication and uncleanness, of doubt and mistrust. Let them be confounded that persecute us; let them perish that hasten to destroy us.

Feria II Rex virginum, amator castitatis et integritatis, caelesti rore benedictionis tuae exstingue in corpore meo fomitem ardentis libidinis, ut maneat in me tenor totius castitatis corporis et animae. Mortifica in membris meis carnis stimulos, omnesque libidinosas commotiones; et da mihi veram et perpetuam castitatem cum ceteris donis tuis, quae tibi placent in veritate, ut sacrificium laudis casto corpore, et mundo corde valeam tibi offerre. Quanta enim cordis contritione et lacrymarum fonte, quanta reverentia et tremore, quanta corporis castitate et animae puritate istud divinum et caeleste sacrificium est celebrandum, ubi Caro tua in veritate sumitur, ubi Sanguis tuus in veritate bibitur, ubi summis ima junguntur, ubi adest sanctorum praesentia Angelorum, ubi tu es sacrificium et Sacerdos mirabiliter et ineffabiliter constitutus!

Monday King of virgins, lover of chastity and integrity, with the heavenly dew of thy blessing quench in my body the tinder of burning lust, that the even course of complete chastity of body and soul may abide in me. Mortify in my members the goads of the flesh and every lustful stirring, and give me true and lasting chastity, with thy other gifts which truly please thee, that I may be able to offer thee the sacrifice of praise with a chaste body and a clean heart. For with what contrition of heart and fount of tears, with what reverence and trembling, with what chastity of body and purity of soul, must this divine and heavenly sacrifice be celebrated, where thy Flesh is truly eaten, where thy Blood is truly drunk, where the lowest is joined to the highest, where the holy Angels are present, where thou art wonderfully and ineffably made both sacrifice and Priest!

Feria III Quis digne hoc celebrare poterit, nisi tu, Deus omnipotens, offerentem feceris dignum? Scio, Domine, et vere scio, et id ipsum tuae pietati confiteor, quia non sum dignus accedere ad tantum mysterium, propter nimia peccata mea, et infinitas negligentias meas. Sed scio, et veraciter, et ex toto corde meo credo, et ore confiteor quia tu potes me facere dignum, qui solus potes facere mundum de immundo conceptum semine, et de peccatoribus justos facis, et sanctos. Per hanc omnipotentiam tuam te rogo, Deus meus, ut concedas mihi peccatori hoc caeleste sacrificium celebrare cum timore et tremore, cum cordis puritate et lacrymarum fonte, cum laetitia spirituali et caelesti gaudio. Sentiat mens mea dulcedinem beatissimae praesentiae tuae, et excubias sanctorum Angelorum tuorum in circuitu meo.

Tuesday Who can celebrate this worthily, unless thou, almighty God, make him who offers it worthy? I know, O Lord, I truly know, and I confess it to thy goodness, that I am not worthy to approach so great a mystery, because of my exceeding sins and countless negligences. But I know, and truly believe with all my heart, and confess with my mouth, that thou canst make me worthy, who alone canst make clean what was conceived of unclean seed, and makest sinners just and holy. By this thine almighty power I pray thee, my God, to grant me, a sinner, to celebrate this heavenly sacrifice with fear and trembling, with purity of heart and a fount of tears, with spiritual gladness and heavenly joy. Let my mind feel the sweetness of thy most blessed presence, and the watch of thy holy Angels round about me.

Feria IV Ego enim, Domine, memor venerandae passionis tuae, accedo ad altare tuum, licet peccator, ut offeram tibi sacrificium, quod tu instituisti, et offerri praecepisti in commemorationem tui pro salute nostra. Suscipe illud, quaeso, summe Deus, pro Ecclesia sancta tua, et pro populo, quem acquisisti Sanguine tuo. Et quoniam me peccatorem inter te et eumdem populum tuum medium esse voluisti, licet in me aliquod boni operis testimonium non agnoscas, officium saltem dispensationis creditae non recuses; nec per me indignum eorum salutis pereat pretium, pro quibus victima salutaris dignatus es esse redemptio. Profero etiam, Domine (si digneris propitius intueri), tribulationes plebium, pericula populorum, captivorum gemitus, miserias orphanorum, necessitates peregrinorum, inopiam debilium, desperationes languentium, defectus senum, suspiria juvenum, vota virginum, lamenta viduarum.

Wednesday For I, O Lord, mindful of thy venerable passion, approach thine altar, sinner though I am, to offer thee the sacrifice which thou didst institute and command to be offered in remembrance of thee for our salvation. Receive it, I pray, most high God, for thy holy Church and for the people whom thou hast purchased with thy Blood. And since thou hast willed that I, a sinner, should stand between thee and that same people of thine, though thou find in me no witness of any good work, refuse not at least the office of the stewardship entrusted to me; nor through me, unworthy, let the price of their salvation be lost, for whom thou didst vouchsafe to be the saving victim and their redemption. I set before thee also, O Lord (if thou wilt look on them in mercy), the troubles of the people, the dangers of the nations, the groans of captives, the miseries of orphans, the needs of travellers, the want of the weak, the despair of the sick, the failing of the old, the sighs of the young, the vows of virgins, the laments of widows.

Feria V Tu enim misereris omnium, Domine, et nihil odisti eorum, quae fecisti. Memorare, quae sit nostra substantia: quia tu Pater noster es, quia tu Deus noster es, ne irascaris satis, neque multitudinem viscerum tuorum super nos contineas. Non enim in justificationibus nostris prosternimus preces ante faciem tuam, sed in miserationibus tuis multis. Aufer a nobis iniquitates nostras, et ignem Sancti Spiritus in nobis clementer accende. Aufer cor lapideum de carne nostra, et da nobis cor carneum, quod te amet, te diligat, in te delectetur, te sequatur, et te perfruatur. Oramus, Domine, clementiam tuam, ut sereno vultu familiam tuam, sacri tui nominis officia praestolantem, aspicere digneris: et ut nullius sit irritum votum, nullius vacua postulatio, tu nobis preces suggere, quas ipse propitius audire et exaudire delecteris.

Thursday For thou hast mercy upon all, O Lord, and hatest none of the things which thou hast made. Remember what our substance is: for thou art our Father, thou art our God; be not exceeding angry, nor shut up the multitude of thy tender mercies from us. For not for our justifications do we lay our prayers before thy face, but for thy many mercies. Take away from us our iniquities, and mercifully kindle in us the fire of the Holy Ghost. Take away the stony heart from our flesh, and give us a heart of flesh, to love thee, to cherish thee, to delight in thee, to follow thee and to enjoy thee. We beseech thy mercy, O Lord, that thou vouchsafe to look with a serene countenance on thy household, waiting upon the offices of thy holy name; and that no one's vow may be in vain and no one's petition empty, do thou inspire in us such prayers as thou thyself art graciously pleased to hear and grant.

Feria VI Rogamus etiam te, Domine sancte Pater, et pro spiritibus fidelium defunctorum, ut sit illis salus, sanitas, gaudium, et refrigerium, hoc magnum pietatis sacramentum. Domine Deus meus, sit illis hodie magnum et plenum convivium de te pane vivo, qui de caelo descendisti, et das vitam mundo; de tua Carne sancta et benedicta, Agni videlicet immaculati, qui tollis peccata mundi, quae de sancto et glorioso Beatae Virginis Mariae utero est assumpta, et de Spiritu Sancto concepta; ac de illo pietatis fonte, qui per lanceam militis ex tuo sacratissimo latere emanavit: ut exinde refecti et satiati, refrigerati et consolati exsultent in laude et gloria tua. Peto clementiam tuam, Domine, ut descendat super panem tibi sacrificandum plenitudo tuae benedictionis, et sanctificatio tuae divinitatis. Descendat etiam, Domine, illa Spiritus Sancti tui invisibilis, incomprehensibilisque majestas, sicut quondam in patrum hostias descendebat, quae et oblationes nostras Corpus et Sanguinem tuum efficiat, et me indignum Sacerdotem doceat tantum tractare mysterium cum cordis puritate et lacrymarum devotione, cum reverentia et tremore; ita ut placite et benigne suscipias sacrificium de manibus meis, ad salutem omnium tam vivorum, quam defunctorum.

Friday We pray thee also, holy Lord and Father, for the spirits of the faithful departed, that this great sacrament of thy love may be to them salvation, health, joy and refreshment. O Lord my God, may there be for them today a great and full banquet of thee, the living bread, who camest down from heaven and givest life to the world; of thy holy and blessed Flesh, the Flesh of the spotless Lamb who takest away the sins of the world, which was taken from the holy and glorious womb of the Blessed Virgin Mary and conceived of the Holy Ghost; and of that fountain of love which flowed from thy most sacred side through the soldier's lance: that, refreshed and filled from it, relieved and consoled, they may exult in thy praise and glory. I ask thy mercy, O Lord, that the fullness of thy blessing and the sanctification of thy Godhead may come down upon the bread to be offered to thee in sacrifice. May there come down also, O Lord, that invisible and incomprehensible majesty of thy Holy Spirit, as of old it came down upon the sacrifices of the fathers, to make our offerings thy Body and Blood, and to teach me, an unworthy priest, to handle so great a mystery with purity of heart and the devotion of tears, with reverence and trembling; so that thou mayest receive the sacrifice from my hands with pleasure and kindness, for the salvation of all, both living and dead.

Sabbato Rogo etiam te, Domine, per ipsum sacrosanctum mysterium Corporis et Sanguinis tui, quo quotidie in Ecclesia tua pascimur et potamur, abluimur et sanctificamur, atque unius divinitatis participes efficimur: da mihi virtutes sanctas, quibus repletus, bona conscientia ad altare tuum accedam; ita ut haec caelestia sacramenta efficiantur mihi salus et vita. Tu enim dixisti ore tuo sancto et benedicto: Panis, quem ego dabo, Caro mea est pro mundi vita. Ego sum panis vivus, qui de caelo descendi. Si quis manducaverit ex hoc pane, vivet in aeternum. Panis dulcissime, sana palatum cordis mei, ut sentiam suavitatem amoris tui. Sana illud ab omni languore, ut nullam praeter te sentiam dulcedinem. Panis candidissime, habens omne delectamentum, et omnem saporem, qui nos semper reficis, et numquam in te deficis: comedat te cor meum, et dulcedine saporis tui repleantur viscera animae meae.

Saturday I pray thee also, O Lord, by this most holy mystery of thy Body and Blood, with which we are daily fed and given to drink in thy Church, washed and sanctified, and made partakers of the one Godhead: give me the holy virtues, filled with which I may approach thine altar with a good conscience, so that these heavenly sacraments may become for me salvation and life. For thou hast said with thy holy and blessed mouth: The bread which I will give is my Flesh for the life of the world. I am the living bread which came down from heaven. If any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever. Most sweet Bread, heal the palate of my heart, that I may taste the sweetness of thy love. Heal it of every weakness, that I may find no sweetness but in thee. Most pure Bread, having every delight and every savour, who ever refreshest us and never failest in thyself: let my heart feed on thee, and the inmost parts of my soul be filled with the sweetness of thy savour.

Manducat te Angelus ore pleno: manducet te peregrinus homo pro modulo suo, ne deficere possit in via, tali recreatus viatico. Panis sancte, panis vive, panis munde, qui descendisti de caelo et das vitam mundo, veni in cor meum, et munda me ab omni inquinamento carnis et spiritus. Intra in animam meam; sana et munda me interius et exterius. Esto tutamen, et continua salus animae, et corporis mei. Repelle a me insidiantes mihi hostes, recedant procul a praesentia potentiae tuae: ut foris et intus per te munitus, recto tramite ad tuum regnum perveniam: ubi non in mysteriis, sicut in hoc tempore agitur, sed facie ad faciem te videbimus, cum tradideris regnum Deo, et Patri, et erit Deus omnia in omnibus. Tunc enim me de te satiabis satietate mirifica; ita ut nec esuriam, nec sitiam in aeternum. Qui cum eodem Deo Patre, et Spiritu Sancto vivis et regnas per omnia saecula saeculorum. Amen.

The Angel feeds on thee with full mouth; let man the pilgrim feed on thee in his small measure, lest he faint by the way, strengthened by such provision for the journey. Holy Bread, living Bread, pure Bread, who camest down from heaven and givest life to the world, come into my heart and cleanse me from every defilement of flesh and spirit. Enter into my soul; heal and cleanse me within and without. Be the defence and the constant health of my soul and body. Drive from me the enemies who lie in wait for me; let them flee far from the presence of thy power; that, protected by thee without and within, I may come by the straight path to thy kingdom; where we shall see thee, not in mysteries as in this present time, but face to face, when thou shalt have delivered up the kingdom to God and the Father, and God shall be all in all. For then thou wilt fill me with thyself with a wondrous fullness, so that I shall neither hunger nor thirst for ever. Who with the same God the Father and the Holy Ghost livest and reignest, world without end. Amen.

Alia oratio ante MissamAnother prayer before Mass

Ad mensam dulcissimi convivii tui, pie Domine Jesu Christe, ego peccator de propriis meritis nihil praesumens, sed de tua confidens misericordia et bonitate, accedere vereor et contremisco. Nam cor et corpus habeo multis criminibus maculatum, mentem et linguam non caute custoditam. Ergo, o pia Deitas, o tremenda majestas, ego miser, inter angustias deprehensus, ad te fontem misericordiae recurro, ad te festino sanandus, sub tuam protectionem fugio, et quem judicem sustinere nequeo, Salvatorem habere suspiro. Tibi, Domine, plagas meas ostendo, tibi verecundiam meam detego. Scio peccata mea multa et magna, pro quibus timeo. Spero in misericordias tuas, quarum non est numerus.

To the table of thy most sweet banquet, loving Lord Jesus Christ, I, a sinner, presuming nothing on my own merits but trusting in thy mercy and goodness, fear and tremble to approach. For I have a heart and body stained with many crimes, a mind and tongue not carefully guarded. Therefore, O loving Godhead, O dread majesty, I, a wretch caught in straits, run back to thee, the fountain of mercy; to thee I hasten to be healed; under thy protection I flee; and him whom I cannot bear as judge I long to have as Saviour. To thee, O Lord, I show my wounds; before thee I uncover my shame. I know that my sins are many and great, and for them I fear. I hope in thy mercies, which are without number.

Respice ergo in me oculis misericordiae tuae, Domine Jesu Christe, Rex aeterne, Deus et homo, crucifixus propter hominem. Exaudi me sperantem in te, miserere mei pleni miseriis et peccatis, tu, qui fontem miserationis numquam manare cessabis. Salve, salutaris victima, pro me et omni humano genere in patibulo crucis oblata. Salve, nobilis et pretiose Sanguis, de vulneribus crucifixi Domini mei Jesu Christi profluens, et peccata totius mundi abluens. Recordare, Domine, creaturae tuae, quam tuo Sanguine redemisti. Poenitet me peccasse: cupio emendare quod feci. Aufer ergo a me, clementissime Pater, omnes iniquitates, et peccata mea; ut purificatus mente et corpore, digne degustare merear Sancta sanctorum; et concede, ut sancta praelibatio Corporis et Sanguinis tui, quam ego indignus sumere intendo, sit peccatorum meorum remissio, sit delictorum perfecta purgatio, sit turpium cogitationum effugatio, ac bonorum sensuum regeneratio, operumque tibi placentium salubris efficacia, animae quoque et corporis contra inimicorum meorum insidias firmissima tuitio. Amen.

Look upon me then with the eyes of thy mercy, Lord Jesus Christ, eternal King, God and man, crucified for man. Hear me who hope in thee; have mercy on me, full of miseries and sins, thou who wilt never cease to let the fountain of compassion flow. Hail, saving Victim, offered on the gibbet of the cross for me and for the whole human race. Hail, noble and precious Blood, flowing from the wounds of my crucified Lord Jesus Christ and washing away the sins of the whole world. Remember, O Lord, thy creature, whom thou hast redeemed with thy Blood. I repent of having sinned; I long to amend what I have done. Take away from me then, most merciful Father, all my iniquities and sins, that, purified in mind and body, I may be worthy to taste the Holy of Holies; and grant that this holy foretaste of thy Body and Blood, which I, unworthy, intend to receive, may be the remission of my sins, the perfect cleansing of my offences, the banishing of shameful thoughts and the renewal of good dispositions, the wholesome fruitfulness of works pleasing to thee, and the strongest defence of soul and body against the snares of my enemies. Amen.

Oratio S. Thomae de Aquino ad Deum PatremPrayer of St Thomas Aquinas to God the Father

Gratias tibi ago, Domine sancte, Pater omnipotens, aeterne Deus, qui me peccatorem, indignum famulum tuum, nullis meis meritis, sed sola dignatione misericordiae tuae, satiare dignatus es pretioso Corpore et Sanguine Filii tui Domini nostri Jesu Christi. Et precor, ut haec sancta communio non sit mihi reatus ad poenam, sed intercessio salutaris ad veniam: Sit mihi armatura fidei, et scutum bonae voluntatis: Sit vitiorum meorum evacuatio, concupiscentiae et libidinis exterminatio, caritatis et patientiae, humilitatis et obedientiae, omniumque virtutum augmentatio; contra insidias inimicorum omnium tam visibilium, quam invisibilium firma defensio; motuum meorum tam carnalium, quam spiritualium perfecta quietatio; in te uno ac vero Deo firmissima adhaesio, atque finis mei felix consummatio. Et precor te, ut ad illud ineffabile convivium me peccatorem perducere digneris, ubi tu cum Filio tuo, et Spiritu Sancto, Sanctis tuis es lux vera, satietas plena, gaudium sempiternum, jucunditas consummata, et felicitas perfecta. Per eumdem Christum Dominum nostrum. Amen.

I give thee thanks, holy Lord, Father almighty, eternal God, who hast vouchsafed, through no merits of mine but by the sole condescension of thy mercy, to feed me, a sinner and thine unworthy servant, with the precious Body and Blood of thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ. And I pray that this holy communion may not be to me a guilt unto punishment, but a saving intercession unto pardon. May it be to me the armour of faith and the shield of good will. May it be the emptying out of my vices, the rooting out of concupiscence and lust, the increase of charity and patience, of humility and obedience, and of all virtues; a firm defence against the snares of all my enemies, visible and invisible; the perfect stilling of all my impulses, both of the flesh and of the spirit; a firm cleaving to thee, the one true God; and the happy fulfilment of my end. And I pray thee to vouchsafe to bring me, a sinner, to that ineffable banquet where thou, with thy Son and the Holy Ghost, art to thy saints true light, full satisfaction, everlasting joy, consummate delight and perfect happiness. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.

Oratio ad Beatissimam Virginem MariamPrayer to the most Blessed Virgin Mary

O serenissima et inclyta Mater Domini nostri Jesu Christi, Virgo Maria, Regina mundi, quae eumdem Creatorem omnium creaturarum in tuo sanctissimo utero fuisti digna portare, cujus idem sacratissimum Corpus et Sanguinem sumpsi: ad ipsum pro me misero peccatore intercedere digneris; ut quidquid in hoc ineffabili Sacramento ignoranter, negligenter, irreverenter, et accidentaliter omisi vel commisi, tuis precibus sanctissimis mihi indulgere dignetur. Qui vivit et regnat.

O most serene and glorious Mother of our Lord Jesus Christ, Virgin Mary, Queen of the world, who wast worthy to bear in thy most holy womb the Creator of all creatures, whose most sacred Body and Blood I have now received: vouchsafe to intercede with him for me, a wretched sinner, that whatever I have omitted or committed in this ineffable Sacrament through ignorance, negligence, irreverence or by accident, he may vouchsafe to forgive me at thy most holy prayers. Who liveth and reigneth.

Rythmus S. Thomae Aquinatis ad sacram EucharistiamThe rhythm of St Thomas Aquinas to the Holy Eucharist

Adoro te devote, latens Deitas,
Quae sub his figuris vere latitas:
Tibi se cor meum totum subjicit,
Quia te contemplans, totum deficit.

I adore thee devoutly, hidden Godhead, who truly liest hidden under these forms: to thee my whole heart submits itself, for in contemplating thee it wholly fails.

Visus, tactus, gustus in te fallitur,
Sed auditu solo tuto creditur:
Credo quidquid dixit Dei Filius,
Nil hoc veritatis verbo verius.

Sight, touch and taste are deceived in thee, but by hearing alone is faith made sure: I believe whatever the Son of God has said; nothing is truer than this word of Truth.

In cruce latebat sola Deitas,
At hic latet simul et humanitas:
Ambo tamen credens, atque confitens,
Peto quod petivit latro poenitens.

On the cross the Godhead alone was hidden, but here the manhood also lies hidden: yet believing and confessing both, I ask what the penitent thief asked.

Plagas, sicut Thomas, non intueor,
Deum tamen meum te confiteor:
Fac me tibi semper magis credere,
In te spem habere, te diligere.

I do not see the wounds, as Thomas did, yet I confess thee my God: make me ever more to believe in thee, to hope in thee, to love thee.

O memoriale mortis Domini,
Panis vivus, vitam praestans homini:
Praesta meae menti de te vivere,
Et te illi semper dulce sapere.

O memorial of the Lord's death, living Bread that givest life to man: grant my mind to live by thee, and ever to taste thy sweetness.

Pie pelicane, Jesu Domine,
Me immundum munda tuo Sanguine;
Cujus una stilla salvum facere
Totum mundum quit ab omni scelere.

Loving pelican, Lord Jesus, cleanse me, unclean, with thy Blood, one drop of which can save the whole world from all its sin.

Jesu, quem velatum nunc aspicio,
Oro fiat illud, quod tam sitio,
Ut te revelata cernens facie,
Visu sim beatus tuae gloriae. Amen.

Jesus, whom now I see veiled, I pray that what I thirst for so may come to pass: that, seeing thee with face unveiled, I may be blessed in the sight of thy glory. Amen.

Oratio devotissima dicenda a Sacerdote ante, vel post celebrationem MissaeA most devout prayer for the priest to say before or after celebrating Mass

Pater caelestis, clementissime Domine, Pater misericordiarum, suscipe hodie per manus servi tui hoc sacrosanctum sacrificium, verum Corpus et Sanguinem unigeniti Filii tui Domini nostri Jesu Christi, in satisfactionem et remissionem omnium peccatorum meorum, in salutem et fortitudinem animae meae, et totius domus meae, et omnium illorum, quibus aliqua obligatione teneor, et omnium dominorum, Praelatorum, Praedicatorum, Sacerdotum, et Religiosorum catholicae fidei, ut eis gratiam Spiritus Sancti impertiri digneris ad salutem animarum suarum, et regimen totius populi christiani; et omnium peccatorum hujus mundi, ut eos convertas, et ducas ad viam salutis; in consolationem omnium tribulatorum, ut eis adjutorium et veram patientiam tribuas; in refrigerium et liberationem omnium animarum exsistentium in Purgatorio, et maxime illarum, quae auxilium a me jure exspectant; et ad illuminationem et conversionem omnium gentium infidelium, haereticorum, et schismaticorum, ut cognoscant et ament te summam veritatem, Patrem omnipotentem, qui cum Filio et Spiritu Sancto es laudabilis Deus, benedictus in saecula saeculorum. Amen.

Heavenly Father, most merciful Lord, Father of mercies, receive today by the hands of thy servant this most holy sacrifice, the true Body and Blood of thine only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ: in satisfaction and remission of all my sins; for the salvation and strength of my soul, and of all my household, and of all those to whom I am bound by any obligation; and of all lords, prelates, preachers, priests and religious of the catholic faith, that thou vouchsafe to impart to them the grace of the Holy Ghost for the salvation of their souls and the government of the whole Christian people; and of all the sinners of this world, that thou mayest convert them and lead them into the way of salvation; for the consolation of all the afflicted, that thou mayest give them help and true patience; for the refreshment and deliverance of all the souls in Purgatory, and above all of those that rightly look to me for help; and for the enlightening and conversion of all unbelieving nations, heretics and schismatics, that they may know and love thee, the supreme truth, the Father almighty, who with the Son and the Holy Ghost art God worthy of praise, blessed for ever and ever. Amen.

Alia oratioAnother prayer

Anima Christi sanctissima, sanctifica me. Corpus Christi sacratissimum, salva me. Sanguis Christi pretiosissime, inebria me. Aqua lateris Christi purissima, munda me. Sudor vultus Christi virtuosissime, sana me. Passio Christi piissima, conforta me. O bone Jesu, custodi me. Intra vulnera tua absconde me. Non permittas me separari a te. Ab hoste maligno defende me. Most powerful Sweat from the face of Christ, heal me. Most loving Passion of Christ, strengthen me. O good Jesus, keep me. Within thy wounds hide me. Suffer me not to be separated from thee.
