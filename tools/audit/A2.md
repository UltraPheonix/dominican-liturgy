## Notes for whoever continues
- The text layer is very reliable for words, capitals and mid-line punctuation, with three exceptions:
  - It drops punctuation at the end of lines near the gutter. `prop.py` therefore now keeps the site's punctuation there (marked "eol?").
  - It reads "í" at the start of a word as "I".
  - It garbles some words; those are marked VAR? and the site's word is kept.
- The 1939 Missal spells cena/cenáculum/cenávit; the site has cœna.
- The coordinator's i/j conversion is still needed site-wide. Expanded conclusions were kept; only their type was compared. Mismatches found: Pent0f Secret (site Per Dóminum, Missal Per eúmdem) and the Pasc3 Collect (the end needs checking). Both are unverified.

## Why these errors existed
- The orations come from Divinum Officium (src "Tempora/…"), which has Roman punctuation: commas around "quǽsumus, Dómine", and "," / "." where the Missal has ";" and ":". They also have Roman spellings: sollemnitáte, cœlésti, fœtu.
- The readings (read from the 1933 scan, tools/m33) contain OCR slips: "prædo. Factus", "man; ducétur", ")ei", a stray "Deus:". They also have lines dropped at column or page breaks (the Passion omissions, the Pasc0t Lesson, the Lesson endings "Dómine Deus noster/meus") and cuts at page breaks (Pasc0h).
