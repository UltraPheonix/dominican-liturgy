## Why these errors existed
- The Commune keys were built from the Graduale's commune pages, and their orations were attached from the Missal or Divinum Officium by approximate title. So some keys took the prayers of a neighbouring Mass (C7s, C39s, C51s).
- Readings read from the 1933 scan dropped the Missal's closing formulas ("In Christo Jesu Domino nostro", "Dominus Deus noster") and normalised the punctuation.
