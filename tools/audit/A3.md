## Conventions followed (from the coordinator)
- Expanded conclusions were kept; only the conclusion type was checked.
- i and j were treated as the same letter (site-wide i→j conversion comes later).
- Calendar-style titles were not changed.
- Capitalisation after ':' (Missal "Et") was not changed, except where a period was inserted.

## Why these errors existed
- The readings come from the 1933 Missal pass (tools/m33), and the punctuation differs in 1939. That pass also cut some text short (S1102 Gospel: a whole clause was missing).
- The DO-sourced orations kept Divinum Officium punctuation and spelling.
- Chants for the 2nd and 3rd Masses of All Souls were copied from the 1st Mass.
