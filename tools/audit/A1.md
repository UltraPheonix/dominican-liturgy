## Why these errors existed
- **Orations:** most A1 orations have src "Tempora/…", i.e. Divinum Officium. They carry the Roman (DO) punctuation, which has a comma after quǽsumus and commas where the Missal prints ';' or ':'. They also carry DO spellings (sollémnia, cœl-, i for j).
- **Readings:** these come from the 1933 OCR (tools/m33). Its builder systematically cut the Missal's ending formulas ("Dicit Dóminus omnípotens.", "Dóminus Deus noster.", "In Christo Jesu Dómino nostro."), cut text at page breaks, and kept OCR punctuation slips.

## Scripts (copied to tools/audit/scripts/A1/; working copies in /tmp/A1)
| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| stream.py | Reads the M39.pdf word layer in reading order, with bounding boxes, into /tmp/A1/stream.json. |
| cmp.py and run.py | Align each site text with the Missal and diff it word by word and punctuation by punctuation. Output: /tmp/A1/diff/KEY.txt, items_all.json and loc_all.json. |
| review.py KEY | Makes a numbered list of differences and scan crops (/tmp/A1/rv/KEY_n.png, via crop.py). |
| build_fix.py | Builds fixes/A1.json from dec.json: per Mass, rejP (rejected item numbers) and edits ([field, old, new, why]). |
| headtail.txt | The text around the start and end of every reading in the Missal. Use it to find cut readings. |

To resume:
1. Run stream.py, then run.py, then `cp items_all.json items_frozen.json`. Item numbers in dec.json refer to items_frozen.json.
2. For each Mass, run `review.py KEY` and look at the crops.
3. Add the Mass to dec.json, then run build_fix.py and `apply_fixes.py --dry`.
