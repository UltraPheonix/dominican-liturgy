# Notes from proofing the orations against the Missale O.P. 1939 (for Joseph)
- S0116b Collect (B. Gundisalvi): addressed to the Father ("sancti nominis tui") but the Missal prints the short conclusion "Qui vivis." Expanded as "Qui vivis et regnas cum Deo Patre..." per the Missal; check whether you want it so.
- S1121 Collect: Missal misprint "presentári" in the second occurrence; printed as "præsentári".
- S0830 Postcommunion: Missal misprint "dápi-pibus" at a line break; printed as "dápibus".
