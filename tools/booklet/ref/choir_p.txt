[G] Source. All melodies follow the Graduale juxta ritum Sacri Ordinis Prædicatorum, ed. Suárez (Rome, 1950); page numbers are given under each heading. Transcriptions (GABC, from GregoBase, gregobase.selapa.net): Officium Gaudeámus, Innocent Smith, OP; Responsorium Timéte, Allelúia Judicábunt and Communio Gaudéte justi, Stephen Gensemer; Offertorium Mirábilis Deus, Andrew Hinkley (final Allelúia omitted, as the rubric directs outside Paschaltide); Kyrie X and Ite X, Matthew Schellhorn; Credo I and the Modus respondendi, Anthony VanBerkum, OP. Gloria, Sanctus and Agnus Dei X: Vatican edition (Mass VIII) transcribed by Andrew Hinkley, collated with Grad. O. P. pp. 153*–158*, where the melody is identical; rhythmic signs are omitted, as in the Dominican book. Glória Patri of the Officium set on the termination printed at p. 611. Art. All images are public-domain works from the open-access collection of the Art Institute of Chicago (CC0): Neri da Rimini, antiphonary leaf with St Dominic in the initial “G” (1310/15); G. B. Tiepolo, Virgin and Child with Saints Dominic and Hyacinth (1730–35); F. Ghissi, The Crucifixion (c. 1370); illuminated border from a Florentine laudario (c. 1340); historiated initials from Rimini (1300/10), France (c. 1300) and by Don Silvestro dei Gherarducci (1392/99); Lo Spagna, St Catherine of Siena; A. Vivarini, St Peter Martyr; Sano di Pietro, Virgin and Child with Saints and Angels; A. Dürer, The Last Supper (1510); M. Raimondi, St Paul Preaching at Athens; J. de Gheyn II, St Matthew; P. Farinati, St John the Evangelist; circle of G. Clovio, The Four Evangelists. Captions give the work shown on each page. Edition. Engraved with Gregorio; set in EB Garamond (Georg Duffner). Colours: Providence Black and Providence Gold (Providence College palette). Prepared for the Solemn Mass of 12 November 2026.
