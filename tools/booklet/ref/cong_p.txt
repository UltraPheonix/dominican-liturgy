Today the Order of Preachers keeps in one feast all its saints and blessed: the friars, nuns, sisters and laity who followed St Dominic in preaching the Gospel for the salvation of souls. The Mass takes its chants from the feast of All Saints; the Collect, Epistle and Gospel are proper to the day, and the Preface is that of our Holy Father St Dominic.
