SOURCES
•    The Dominican Rite calendar that Fr. Augustine Thompson,
     OP, prepares every year and oﬀers on his blog, dominican-
     liturgy.blogspot.com. As such, it is specific to the Western
     Dominican Province in the United States.
•    Page numbers from scans of the 1933, 1939, and 1965 Missale
     Iuxta Ritum Ordinis Prædicatorum Dominican Rite Altar Missals
     available on Fr. Augustine’s blog (“M33”, “M39”, “M65”).

INDICATIONS
•   Each entry includes an indication for:
         o    Gloria
         o    Credo
         o    Alleluia reminders:
                    §    Easter ferials:
                              •      Mon, Wed, Fri: "1st Alleluia"
                              •      Tue, Thu, Sat: "2nd Alleluia"
                    §    Other ferials: "No Alleluia".

FERIAS
•   Page numbers given for ferial days outside of Advent and Lent
    are of the previous Sunday.

LOCAL FEASTS
•  There are multiple entries for the same day for:
        o     feasts proper to the dioceses and houses of the
              Western Province
        o     BVM, Refuge of Sinners, (CA only)
        o     Western Dominican Province feasts,
        o     USA feasts,
•  In the back of the Ordo is a listing of feasts proper to the other
   Dominican Provinces in the United States, as well as the
   Diocese of Fairbanks, residence of Bp. Stephen Maekawa, OP.
•    On the anniversary of a priest’s ordination, the collect to add
     (with a single conclusion) commemorating this event can be
     found on:
          o     M33: 81*, M39: 104*, M65: [85]

SUPPLEMENTS
•   Missal page numbers listed are as if the user has no supplements
    or additions of propers or prefaces to the missal he is using, with
    a note to refer to the back of the Ordo on days when one may
    exist.

ABBREVIATIONS
•  M33, M39, M65: The Dominican Rite Altar Missals published
   in 1933, 1939, and 1965, respectively
•  Mem: Memorial
•  Comm: Commemoration (used to indicate an addition to the
   collect on the day of installation of a pope or bishop)
•  BVM: Blessed Virgin Mary
•  M: Martyr (Mm: Many Martyrs)
•  P: Pope
•  V: Virgin (Vv: Many Virgins)
•  D: Doctor
•  Abb: Abbot
•  C: Confessor
•  E: Bishop
•  Matr: Matron
•  Vid: Widow
•  Ap: Apostle (App: Many Apostles)
•  R: King
