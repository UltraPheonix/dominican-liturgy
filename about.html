About this site

This website is dedicated to Peter Bratschi, Josef Crenshaw, and Father Gregory Santy, who have all put up with me, and have worked diligently to bring the Dominican rite to Providence College. This would not have been possible without them.

The Mass and Office of the Dominican Rite, with their chant, for any day.

This is to be used for private devotion and as a useful resource, not as an authoritative liturgical text. Please consult actual liturgical texts if celebrating the Dominican rite.

I am not affiliated with the Order of Preachers. This is a private project. It is not made, approved or endorsed by the Dominican Order or any of its provinces, and it is not an official liturgical book.

Books, and supporting the Order

The liturgical books of the Dominican Rite are in print from Dominican Liturgy Publications, the bookstore linked from the Dominican Liturgy blog. Please purchase books from this bookstore to support the Order.

  • Graduale juxta ritum Sacri Ordinis Prædicatorum (1950, ed. Suarez)
  • Epistles and Gospels of the Year according to the Dominican Rite
  • Dominican Rite Mass Ordo (2026)
  • Dominican Rite Mass Pew Booklet
  • W. Bonniwell O.P., Dominican Ceremonial for Mass and Benediction (1946)
  • Tonorum communium Regulae (1965, ed. Fernandez)
  • Cantica Evangelica secundum cantum Ordinis Prædicatorum
  • Ad Completorium
  • Libellus Precum
  • Dominican Altar Boys' Manual (1945)

To support the friars directly, you can give to the Dominican Province of St. Joseph (the Eastern Province of the United States) through the Dominican Friars Foundation. This site has no connection with either the bookstore or the Province, and receives nothing from them.
