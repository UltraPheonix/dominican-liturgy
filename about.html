About this site

This site shows the sung propers of the Dominican Rite Mass (Introit, Gradual, Alleluia or Tract, Sequence, Offertory, Communion) for any day, in square notation, from the Graduale juxta ritum Sacri Ordinis Praedicatorum (Rome, 1950, ed. Suarez). Each chant can be changed, edited as GABC, printed or saved as a PDF, and shared as a link.

Where the chants come from

All 975 chants of the 1950 Graduale were transcribed into GABC by volunteers for GregoBase; each chant on this site names its transcriber and links to its GregoBase page. One chant (the Alleluia Laetatus sum, p. 5) is still empty there.

How each day's Mass was put together

The Graduale prints each chant once, in the order of the year; when a later Mass uses the same chant, the book only refers back to it. The assignments were therefore built in three steps, and the site marks how sure each one is:

  • Proper of the Time. Each Mass in the Graduale was matched to its day by its Introit. Where the Dominican Mass differs from the Roman one (e.g. Memento nostri on the 4th Sunday of Advent), it was checked against the Missale O.P. (1939). A chant marked re-used is not printed with that Mass; it was found elsewhere in the Graduale by the incipit the day has in the Roman Graduale.
  • Proper of the Saints. The feasts, dates and ranks are those of the Calendarium O.P. reformatum (1960 rubrics). Proper: the Mass printed in the Sanctorale of the Graduale, matched to its date through the Missale O.P. From the Common: the Missale names the Common Mass for that day. Common: check Ordo: the Common was chosen from the saint's title (Confessor, Virgin, Martyr…) and should be checked against the Ordo.
  • Commons and votive Masses are as printed in the Graduale (pp. 1*–125*).

Saints' Masses often have proper chants that the Graduale prints on the pages of a nearby feast; these are listed under “Other chants printed on these pages” and can be added with one click. Precedence between a Sunday and a feast is not calculated: follow the Ordo.
