About this site

This site shows the sung propers of the Dominican Rite Mass (Introit, Gradual, Alleluia or Tract, Sequence, Offertory, Communion) for any day, in square notation, from the Graduale juxta ritum Sacri Ordinis Praedicatorum (Rome, 1950, ed. Suarez). Each chant can be changed, edited as GABC, printed or saved as a PDF, and shared as a link.

Where the chants come from

All 975 chants of the 1950 Graduale were transcribed into GABC by volunteers for GregoBase; each chant on this site names its transcriber and links to its GregoBase page. One chant (the Alleluia Laetatus sum, p. 5) is still empty there.

How each day's Mass was put together

The Graduale prints each chant once, in the order of the year, and refers back to it when it is used again. So every Mass was assembled from three books and checked against each other:

  • Proper of the Time. Each Mass of the Graduale was matched to its day by its Introit, then checked against the St. Dominic Missal (1959), which supplies any chant the Graduale only refers back to.
  • Proper of the Saints. The feasts, dates and ranks are those of the Calendarium O.P. (1960 rubrics). The chants are those the St. Dominic Missal assigns ("Mass: No. 13 except…" gives the Common and the proper parts), or, where it could not be read, the page of the Missale O.P. (1939) that the 2026 Ordo cites.
  • The Mass of the day is chosen by class, as in the rubrics of 1960, and agrees with the 2026 Ordo O.P. on the days it was checked against. Saturdays without a feast have the Mass of Our Lady.
  • Seasons. From Septuagesima a saint's Alleluia gives way to the Tract; in Paschaltide the Gradual gives way to two Alleluias, and the optional T.P. Alleluia is sung only then.

A chant marked Roman melody is not in the Graduale O.P. on GregoBase and is shown from the Roman Graduale until it is transcribed. Readings come from the Vulgate and the Douay-Rheims; the English of the chants on the congregation sheet is from the St. Dominic Missal.

Printing

Choir PDF prints the chants of the Mass (and the Kyriale, if chosen) in square notation. Congregation PDF prints every proper and reading in Latin with the English beside it. Both open your device's print dialog: choose “Save as PDF”.
