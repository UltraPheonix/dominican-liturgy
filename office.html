✠ Officium Divinum juxta ritum Sacri Ordinis Praedicatorum
Mass Office About

The spoken Office, in Latin and English. The psalms are given whole from the Vulgate with the Douay-Rheims; the chant will follow. This first edition has Compline and the little hours (Prime, Terce, Sext, None); Matins, Lauds and Vespers are in preparation.
